## Abstract

## 摘要

Point clouds of 3D objects exhibit an inherent compositional nature where simple parts can be assembled into progressively more complex shapes to form whole objects. Explicitly capturing such part-whole hierarchy is a long-sought objective in order to build effective models, but its tree-like nature has made the task elusive. In this paper, we propose to embed the features of a point cloud classifier into the hyperbolic space and explicitly regularize the space to account for the part-whole hierarchy. The hyperbolic space is the only space that can successfully embed the tree-like nature of the hierarchy. This leads to substantial improvements in the performance of state-of-art supervised models for point cloud classification.

三维物体的点云天然具有组合性:简单的部分可以被拼装成越来越复杂的形状，从而形成完整的物体。为了构建有效的模型，明确刻画这种“部分-整体”的层级结构一直是长期追求的目标，但其呈树状的特性使得该任务难以实现。在本文中，我们提出将点云分类器的特征嵌入到双曲空间中，并对该空间进行显式正则化，以刻画部分-整体层级。双曲空间是唯一能够成功嵌入这种树状层级的空间。这带来了对现有最先进的有监督点云分类模型性能的显著提升。

## 1 Introduction

## 1 引言

Is the whole more than the sum of its parts? While philosophers have been debating such deep question since the time of Aristotle, we can certainly say that understanding and capturing the relationship between parts as constituents of whole complex structures is of paramount importance in building models of reality. In this paper, we turn our attention to the compositional nature of 3D objects, represented as point clouds, where simple parts can be assembled to form progressively more complex shapes. Indeed, the complex geometry of an object can be better understood by unraveling the implicit hierarchy of its parts. Such hierarchy can be intuitively captured by a tree where nodes close to the root represent basic universal shapes, which become progressively more complex as we approach the whole-object leaves. Transforming an object into another requires swapping parts by traversing the tree up to a common ancestor part. It is thus clear that a model extracting features, that claim to capture the nature of 3D objects, needs to incorporate such hierarchy.

整体是否大于其各部分之和？自亚里士多德时代起，哲学家们就一直在争论这样一个深刻的问题。但我们可以肯定的是，在构建对现实的模型时，理解并捕捉“部分作为整体复杂结构的组成单元”之间的关系至关重要。本文关注三维物体的组合性:它们可表示为点云，其中简单的部分能够被拼装，从而形成越来越复杂的形状。事实上，通过揭示物体各部分之间隐含的层级结构，可以更好地理解其复杂几何。这样的层级可直观地用一棵树来表示:靠近根节点的节点代表基本的通用形状，而当我们向整体物体的“叶子”靠近时，这些形状会逐渐变得更复杂。将一个物体变换为另一个物体，需要沿着这棵树向上遍历到共同的祖先部分来交换部件。因此，一个用于提取特征、并声称能够捕捉三维物体本质的模型，就必须纳入这种层级结构。

In the last years, point cloud processing methods have tried to devise methods to extract complex geometric information from points and neighborhoods. Architectures like graph neural networks [1] compose the features extracted by local receptive fields, with sophisticated geometric priors [2] exploiting locality and self-similarity, while a different school of thought argues that simple architectures, such as PointMLP [3] and SimpleView [4], with limited geometric priors are nevertherless very effective. It thus raises a question whether prior knowledge about the data is being exploited effectively.

在过去几年里，点云处理方法一直尝试提出方案，从点及其邻域中提取复杂的几何信息。诸如图神经网络 [1] 这类架构，通过局部感受野来组合提取到的特征，并借助更精巧的几何先验 [2] 来利用局部性与自相似性；而另一派观点则认为，即便几何先验有限，像 PointMLP [3] 和 SimpleView [4] 这类简单架构依然非常有效。因此，问题在于:对数据的先验知识是否得到了有效利用？

In this sense, works such as PointGLR [5], Info3D [6] and DCGLR [7] recognized the need to reason about local and global interactions in the feature extraction process. In particular, their claim is that maximizing the mutual information between parts and whole objects leads to understanding of local and global relations. Although these methods present compelling results for unsupervised feature extraction, they still fall short of providing significant improvements when finetuned with supervision.

从这个意义上说，诸如 PointGLR [5]、Info3D [6] 和 DCGLR [7] 的工作都认识到，需要在特征提取过程中同时考虑局部与全局的交互。特别地，它们的观点是:最大化部分与整体对象之间的互信息能够带来对局部与全局关系的理解。尽管这些方法在无监督特征提取上展现了令人信服的结果，但当在监督条件下进行微调时，它们仍难以带来显著的性能提升。

In our work, we argue that those methods do not fulfill their promise of capturing the part-whole relationship because they are unable to represent the tree-like nature of the compositional hierarchy. Indeed, their fundamental weakness lies in the use of spaces that are either flat (Euclidean) or with positive curvature (spherical). However, it is known that only spaces with negative curvature (hyperbolic) are able to embed tree structures with low distortion [8]. This is due to the fact that the volume of the Euclidean space grows only as a power of its radius rather than exponentially, limiting the representation capacity of tree-like data with an exponential number of leaves. This unique characteristic has inspired many researchers to represent hierarchical relations in many domains, from natural language processing [9],[10] to computer vision [11],[12]. However, the use of such principles for point clouds and 3D data is still unexplored.

在我们的工作中，我们认为这些方法没有兑现其捕捉“部分-整体”关系的承诺，因为它们无法表征组合层级的树状特性。事实上，它们的根本弱点在于使用的空间要么是平坦的(欧几里得)，要么是具有正曲率的(球面)。然而，人们已知，只有负曲率(双曲)的空间才能以低失真嵌入树结构 [8]。原因在于:欧几里得空间的体积增长仅与其半径的幂次相关，而不是指数增长，这限制了对包含指数数量叶节点的树状数据的表示能力。正是这一独特特性，启发了许多研究者在诸多领域中用来表示层级关系:从自然语言处理 [9],[10] 到计算机视觉 [11],[12]。然而，将这类思想用于点云和三维数据仍未被充分探索。

The main contributions of this paper lie in the following aspects:

本文的主要贡献体现在以下方面:

- we propose a novel regularizer to supervised training of point cloud classification models that promotes the part-whole hierarchy of compositionality in the hyperbolic space;

- 我们提出一种新型正则化器，用于对点云分类模型的监督训练，促进在双曲空间中组合性的部分-整体层次结构；

- this regularizer can be applied to any state-of-art architecture with a simple modification of its head to perform classification with hyperbolic layers in the regularized space, coupled with Riemannian optimization [13];

- 该正则化器可应用于任何最先进的架构，只需对其头部进行简单修改，使其在正则化空间中使用双曲层完成分类，并结合黎曼优化 [13]；

- we observe a significant improvement in the performance of a number of popular architectures, including state-of-the-art techniques, surpassing the currently known best results on two different datasets;

- 我们观察到多种流行架构(包括最先进技术)的性能显著提升，在两个不同数据集上超越了目前已知的最佳结果；

- we are the first to experimentally observe the desired part-whole hierarchy, by noticing that the geodesics in hyperbolic space between whole objects pass though common part ancestors.

- 我们是首个通过实验观察到期望的部分-整体层次结构:注意到双曲空间中连接整体对象的测地线，会穿过共同的部分祖先。

## 2 Related work

## 2 相关工作

Point Cloud Analysis Point cloud data are sets of multiple points and, in recent years, several deep neural networks have been studied to process them. Early works adapted models for images through 2D projections [14], [15]. Later, PointNet [16] established new models working directly on the raw set of 3D coordinates by exploiting shared architectures invariant to points permutation. Originally, PointNet independently processed individual points through a shared MLP. To improve performance, PointNet++ [17] exploited spatial correlation by using a hierarchical feature learning paradigm. Other methods [18], [19], [20], treat point clouds as a graph and exploit operators defined over irregular sets to capture relations among points and their neighbors at different resolutions. This is the case of DGCNN [21], where the EdgeConv graph convolution operation aggregates features supported on neighborhoods as defined by a nearest neighbor graph dynamically computed in the feature space. Recently, PointMLP [3] revisits PointNet++ to include the concept of residual connections. Through this simple model, the authors show that sophisticated geometric models are not essential to obtain state-of-the-art performance.

点云分析 点云数据是由多个点组成的集合，近年来已经研究了若干深度神经网络来处理它们。早期工作通过二维投影将模型改造成适用于图像 [14]，[15]。随后，PointNet [16] 通过利用对点置换不变的共享架构，建立了直接在原始三维坐标集合上工作的新模型。最初，PointNet 通过共享的 MLP 独立处理每个点。为提升性能，PointNet++ [17] 通过使用层次化特征学习范式来利用空间相关性。其他方法 [18]，[19]，[20] 将点云视为图，并利用定义在不规则集合上的算子，在不同分辨率下捕获点及其邻居之间的关系。这正是 DGCNN [21] 的做法:EdgeConv 图卷积操作会汇聚由在特征空间中动态计算的最近邻图所定义的邻域所支持的特征。最近，PointMLP [3] 回顾并改进 PointNet++，引入残差连接的概念。通过这个简单模型，作者表明，要达到最先进的性能并不必需复杂的几何模型。

Part Compositionality Successfully capturing the semantics of 3D objects represented as point clouds requires to learn interactions between local and global information, and, in particular, the compositional nature of 3D objects as constructed from local parts. Indeed, some works have focused on capturing global-local reasoning in point cloud processing. One of the first and most representative works is PointGLR [5]. In this work, the authors map local features at different levels within the network to a common hypersphere where the global features embedding is made close to such local embeddings. This is the first approach towards modeling the similarities of parts (local features) and whole objects (global features). The use of a hypersphere as embedding space for similarity promotion traces its roots in metric learning works for face recognition [22]. In addition to the global-local embedding, PointGLR added two other pretext tasks, namely normal estimation and self-reconstruction, to further promote learning of highly discriminative features. Our work significantly differs from PointGLR in multiple ways: i) a positive curvature manifold such as the hypersphere is unable to accurately embed hierarchies (tree-like structures), hence our adoption of the hyperbolic space; ii) we actively promote a continuous embedding of part-whole hierarchies by penalizing the hyperbolic norm of parts proportionally to their number of points (a proxy for part complexity); iii) we move the classification head of the model to the hyperbolic space to exploit our regularized geometry. A further limitation of PointGLR is the implicit assumption of a model generating progressive hierarchies (e.g. via expanding receptive fields) in the intermediate layers. In contrast, our work can be readily adopted by any state-of-the-art model with just a replacement of the final layers. Other works revisit the global-local relations using maximization of mutual information between different views [6], clustering and contrastive learning [23], distillation with constrast [7], self-similarity and contrastive learning with hard negative samples [24]. Although most of these works include the contrastive strategy, they differ in the way they contrast the positive and negative samples and in the details of the self-supervision procedures, e.g., contrastive loss and point cloud augmentations. We also notice that most these works focus on unsupervised learning, and, while they show that the features learned in this manner are highly discriminative, they are also mostly unable to improve upon state-of-the-art supervised methods when finetuned with full supervision. These approaches differ from the one followed in this paper, where we focus on regularization of a fully supervised method, and we show improvements upon the supervised baselines that do not adopt our regularizer.

部分组合性 成功捕捉以点云表示的三维对象的语义，需要学习局部信息与全局信息之间的交互，尤其是，学习三维对象由局部部件构建而成的组合性质。确实，有些工作聚焦于点云处理中捕捉全局-局部推理。最早且最具代表性的工作之一是 PointGLR [5]。在该工作中，作者将网络内不同层级的局部特征映射到一个共同的超球面:全局特征嵌入被设计得与这些局部嵌入彼此接近。这是首个用于刻画部件(局部特征)与整体对象(全局特征)相似性的方案。使用超球面作为相似性促进的嵌入空间，其根源可追溯到用于人脸识别的度量学习工作 [22]。除了全局-局部嵌入之外，PointGLR 还加入了另外两个预训练任务，即法向估计和自重建，以进一步促进学习高度具有区分性的特征。我们的工作在多个方面与 PointGLR 显著不同:i) 像超球面这样的正曲率流形无法准确嵌入层次结构(树状结构)，因此我们采用双曲空间；ii) 我们通过惩罚双曲范数，使部件的嵌入连续地保持部分-整体层次，并且该惩罚与部件所包含的点数成比例(作为部分复杂度的代理)；iii) 我们将模型的分类头移动到双曲空间中，以利用我们所正则化的几何结构。PointGLR 的另一个局限在于它隐含假设模型在中间层生成逐步的层次(例如通过扩展感受野)。相反，我们的工作可以很容易地被任何最先进模型采用，只需替换最终层即可。其他工作通过最大化不同视图之间的互信息 [6]、聚类与对比学习 [23]、使用对比进行蒸馏 [7]、采用硬负样本的自相似性与对比学习 [24] 来重新审视全局-局部关系。尽管这些工作多数都包含对比策略，但它们在区分正负样本的方式以及自监督流程的细节上有所不同，例如对比损失与点云增强。我们还注意到，这些工作大多关注无监督学习，尽管它们表明以这种方式学到的特征具有很强的区分性，但在用完整监督进行微调时，通常也大多无法在监督最先进方法上进一步提升。与本文所采用的方法不同，我们关注对一种完全监督方法进行正则化，并表明我们的正则化器能够带来相较于未采用该正则化器的监督基线的改进。

Hyperbolic Learning The intuition that the hyperbolic space is crucial to embed hierarchical structures comes from the work of Sarkar [8] who proved that trees can be embedded in the hyperbolic space with arbitrarily low distortion. This inspired several works which investigated how various frameworks of representation learning can be reformulated in non-Euclidean manifolds. In particular, [9] [13] and [10] were some of the first works to explore hyperbolic representation learning by introducing Riemannian adaptive optimization, Poincarè embeddings and hyperbolic neural networks for natural language processing. The new mathematical formalism introduced by Ganea et al. [10] was decisive to demonstrate the effectiveness of hyperbolic variants of neural network layers compared to the Euclidean counterparts. Generalizations to other data, such as images [25] and graphs [26] with the corresponding hyperbolic variants of the main operations like graph convolution [26] and gyroplane convolution [12] have also been studied. In the context of unsupervised learning, new objectives in the hyperbolic space force the models to include the implicit hierarchical structure of the data leading to a better clustering in the embedding space [12], [11]. To the best of our knowledge, no work has yet focused on hyperbolic representations for point clouds. Indeed, 3D objects present an intrinsic hierarchy where whole objects are made by parts of different size. While the smallest parts may be shared across different object classes, the larger the parts the more class-specific they become. This consistently fits with the structure of a tree where simple fundamental parts are shared ancestors of complex objects and hence we show how the hyperbolic space can fruitfully capture this data prior.

双曲学习:双曲空间对于嵌入层次结构至关重要这一直觉，来自 Sarkar [8] 的工作；他证明了树可以以任意低的失真嵌入到双曲空间中。这启发了多项研究，考察如何将各种表征学习框架改写为非欧几里得流形上的形式。特别是，[9] [13] 和 [10] 是最早一批通过引入黎曼自适应优化、Poincarè 嵌入以及用于自然语言处理的双曲神经网络来探索双曲表征学习的工作。Ganea 等人 [10] 引入的新数学形式，对于证明相较于欧几里得版本，神经网络层的双曲变体具有有效性至关重要。除主要操作的双曲变体外，还研究了其他数据的推广，例如图像 [25] 和图 [26]，以及与之对应的主要操作的双曲变体，如图卷积 [26] 和陀螺平面卷积 [12]。在无监督学习的背景下，双曲空间中的新目标迫使模型纳入数据的隐式层次结构，从而在嵌入空间中实现更好的聚类 [12]，[11]。据我们所知，目前还没有工作专注于用于点云的双曲表征。事实上，3D 物体具有内在层次:整个物体由不同尺寸的部件构成。尽管最小的部件可能在不同物体类别之间共享，但部件越大，就越具有类别特异性。这与树的结构始终一致:简单的基础部件是复杂物体的共享祖先。因此，我们展示了双曲空间如何有益地捕捉这种数据 prior。

## 3 Method

## 3 方法

In this section we present our proposed method, named HyCoRe (Hyperbolic Compositional Regularizer). An overview is presented in Fig. 1. At a high level, HyCoRe enhances any state-of-the-art neural network model for point cloud classification by 1) replacing its last layers with layers performing transformations in the hyperbolic space (see Sec. 3.2), and 2) regularizing the classification loss to induce a desirable configuration of the hyperbolic feature space where embeddings of parts both follow a hierarchy and cluster according to class labels.

在本节中，我们提出一种方法，命名为 HyCoRe(Hyperbolic Compositional Regularizer)。总体概览如图 1 所示。概括而言，HyCoRe 通过 1)将其最后几层替换为在双曲空间中执行变换的层(见第 3.2 节)，以及 2)对分类损失进行正则化，以诱导双曲特征空间中的理想配置:部件的嵌入既遵循层次结构，又能根据类别标签进行聚类，从而增强任意最先进的点云分类神经网络模型。

### 3.1 Compositional Hierarchy in 3D Point Clouds

### 3.1 3D 点云中的组合层次结构

The objective of HyCoRe is to regularize the feature space produced by a neural network so that it captures the compositional structure of the 3D point cloud at different levels. In particular, we notice that there exists a hierarchy where small parts (e.g., simple structures like disks, squares, triangles) composed of few points are universal ancestors to more complex shapes included in many different objects. As these structures are composed into more complex parts with more points, they progressively become more specific to an object or class. This hierarchy can be mathematically represented by a tree, as depicted in Fig 2 where a simple cylinder can be the ancestor of both pieces of a chair or a table. While the leaves in the tree are whole objects, thus belonging to a specific class, their ancestors are progressively more universal the higher up in the hierarchy they sit.

HyCoRe 的目标是对神经网络产生的特征空间进行正则化，使其在不同层级上捕捉 3D 点云的组合结构。具体而言，我们注意到存在一种层次:由少量点构成的小部件(例如简单结构，如圆盘、方形、三角形)是许多不同物体中所包含的更复杂形状的通用祖先。随着这些结构被组合成包含更多点的更复杂部件，它们会逐步变得更特定于某个物体或类别。该层次可以用一棵树在数学上表示，如图 2 所示，一个简单的圆柱既可以是椅子部件也可以是桌子部件的祖先。尽管树的叶节点对应的是完整物体，因此属于特定类别，但它们的祖先随着在层次结构中位置越靠上而变得越来越通用。

At this point, it is important noting that the graph distance between leaves is determined by the shortest path passing through the first common ancestor for objects in the same or similar classes, while objects from significantly dissimilar classes have the shortest path passing through the root of the hierarchy. In order to ensure that we can embed this tree structure in a feature space, we need a space that preserves the geometrical properties of trees and especially the graph distance. In particular, the embedding space must be able to accommodate the exponential volume growth of a tree along its radius. A classic result by Sarkar [8] showed that flat Euclidean space does not provide this, leading to high errors when embedding trees, even in high dimensions. On the contrary, the hyperbolic space, a Riemannian manifold with negative curvature, does support exponentially increasing volumes and can embed trees with arbitrarily low distortion. Indeed, the geodesic (shortest path) between two points in this space does pass through points closer to the origin, mimicking the behavior of distance defined over a tree.

此时，必须注意:叶节点之间的图距离由最短路径决定，该最短路径会经过同类或相似类别物体的第一个共同祖先；而对于显著不相似的类别，最短路径则会经过层次结构的根。为了确保我们能够将这棵树结构嵌入到特征空间中，我们需要一个空间来保留树的几何性质，尤其是图距离。具体来说，嵌入空间必须能够容纳树在其半径方向上指数式增长的体积。Sarkar [8] 的经典结论表明，平坦的欧几里得空间无法做到这一点，因此即使在高维情况下对树进行嵌入也会产生高误差。相反，具有负曲率的黎曼流形——双曲空间——支持指数增长的体积，并且能够以任意低的失真嵌入树。事实上，在该空间中任意两点之间的测地线(最短路径)会经过更接近原点的点，从而模仿了在树上定义的距离的行为。

In particular, we will focus on the Poincarè ball model of hyperbolic space. Since hyperbolic space is a non-Euclidean manifold, it cannot benefit from conventional vector representations and linear algebra. As a consequence, classical neural networks cannot operate in such a space. However, we will use extensions [10] of classic layers defined through the concept of gyrovector spaces.

特别地，我们将重点关注双曲空间的庞加莱球模型。由于双曲空间是一个非欧几里得流形，它无法从传统的向量表示和线性代数中受益。因此，经典神经网络无法在这种空间中运行。然而，我们将使用通过陀螺向量空间概念定义的经典层的扩展[10]。

### 3.2 Hyperbolic Space and Neural Networks

### 3.2 双曲空间与神经网络

The hyperbolic space is a Riemannian manifold with constant negative curvature. The curvature determines the metric of a space by the following formula:

双曲空间是一个具有恒定负曲率的黎曼流形。曲率通过下式决定空间的度量:

\[
{\mathbf{g}}_{R} = {\left( {\lambda }_{x}^{c}\right) }^{2}{\mathbf{g}}_{E} = \frac{2}{1 + c\parallel \mathbf{x}{\parallel }^{2}}{\mathbf{g}}_{E} \tag{1}
\]

where \( {\mathbf{g}}_{R} \) is the metric tensor of a generic Riemannian manifold, \( {\lambda }_{x}^{c} \) is the conformal factor that depends on the curvature \( c \) and on the point \( \mathbf{x} \) on which is calculated, and \( {\mathbf{g}}_{E} \) is the metric tensor of the Euclidean space \( {\mathbb{R}}^{n} \) , i.e., the identity tensor \( {\mathbf{I}}_{n} \) . Note how the metric depends on the coordinates (through \( \parallel \mathbf{x}\parallel \) ) for \( c \neq  0 \) , and how \( c = 0 \) yields \( {\mathbf{g}}_{R} = 2{\mathbf{g}}_{E} \) , i.e., the Euclidean space is a flat Riemannian manifold with zero curvature. Spaces with \( c > 0 \) are spherical, and with \( c < 0 \) hyperbolic.

其中，\( {\mathbf{g}}_{R} \) 是一般黎曼流形的度量张量，\( {\lambda }_{x}^{c} \) 是与曲率 \( c \) 以及用于计算的点 \( \mathbf{x} \) 有关的共形因子，\( {\mathbf{g}}_{E} \) 是欧几里得空间 \( {\mathbb{R}}^{n} \) 的度量张量，即恒等张量 \( {\mathbf{I}}_{n} \)。注意度量如何通过 \( \parallel \mathbf{x}\parallel \)(对 \( c \neq  0 \))而依赖于坐标，以及 \( c = 0 \) 如何得到 \( {\mathbf{g}}_{R} = 2{\mathbf{g}}_{E} \)，也就是说，欧几里得空间是一个具有零曲率的平坦黎曼流形。具有 \( c > 0 \) 的空间是球面的，而具有 \( c < 0 \) 的空间是双曲的。

The Poincarè Ball in \( n \) dimensions \( {\mathbb{D}}^{n} \) is a hyperbolic space with \( c =  - 1 \) , and it is isometric to other models such as the Lorentz model. The distance and norm are defined as:

在 \( n \) 维空间 \( {\mathbb{D}}^{n} \) 中的庞加莱球是一个具有 \( c =  - 1 \) 的双曲空间，并且与洛伦兹模型等其他模型等距。距离与范数定义为:

\[
{d}_{\mathbb{D}}\left( {\mathbf{x},\mathbf{y}}\right)  = {\cosh }^{-1}\left( {1 + 2\frac{\parallel \mathbf{x} - \mathbf{y}{\parallel }^{2}}{\left( {1 - \parallel \mathbf{x}{\parallel }^{2}}\right) \left( {1 - \parallel \mathbf{y}{\parallel }^{2}}\right) }}\right) ,\;\parallel \mathbf{x}{\parallel }_{\mathbb{D}} = 2{\tanh }^{-1}\left( {\parallel \mathbf{x}\parallel }\right) \tag{2}
\]

![bo_d7b5esalb0pc73dd88fg_4_381_205_1034_545_0.jpg](images/bo_d7b5esalb0pc73dd88fg_4_381_205_1034_545_0.jpg)

The hyperbolic space can embed trees and hierarchical structures with lower distortions than the Euclidean space. The number of points in the embedded part point cloud is highlighted in figure. Embeddings shown are experimental results projected to 2D Poincarè disk with hyperbolic UMAP.

图2:3D 物体由于其作为小部分组合的本质，具有内在层级结构。与欧几里得空间相比，双曲空间可以以更低的失真嵌入树和层级结构。嵌入部分点云中的点数量在图中突出显示。所示嵌入是将结果投影到具有双曲 UMAP 的 2D 庞加莱圆盘上的实验结果。

Since the Poincarè Ball is a Riemannian manifold, for each point \( \mathbf{x} \in  {\mathbb{D}}^{n} \) we can define a logarithmic map \( {\log }_{\mathbf{x}} : {\mathbb{D}}^{n} \rightarrow  {T}_{\mathbf{x}}{\mathbb{D}}^{n} \) that maps points from the Poincarè Ball to the corresponding tangent space \( {T}_{\mathbf{x}}{\mathbb{D}}^{n} \in  {\mathbb{R}}^{n} \) , and an exponential map \( {\exp }_{\mathbf{x}} : {T}_{\mathbf{x}}{\mathbb{D}}^{n} \rightarrow  {\mathbb{D}}^{n} \) that does the opposite. These operations [10] are fundamental to move from one space to the other and viceversa.

由于庞加莱球是一个黎曼流形，对于任意点 \( \mathbf{x} \in  {\mathbb{D}}^{n} \)，我们都可以定义一个对数映射 \( {\log }_{\mathbf{x}} : {\mathbb{D}}^{n} \rightarrow  {T}_{\mathbf{x}}{\mathbb{D}}^{n} \)，将庞加莱球上的点映射到对应的切空间 \( {T}_{\mathbf{x}}{\mathbb{D}}^{n} \in  {\mathbb{R}}^{n} \)；并定义一个指数映射 \( {\exp }_{\mathbf{x}} : {T}_{\mathbf{x}}{\mathbb{D}}^{n} \rightarrow  {\mathbb{D}}^{n} \)，做相反的操作。这些操作[10]是从一个空间移动到另一个空间及反向的基础。

The formalism to generalize tensor operations in the hyperbolic space is called the gyrovector space, where addition, scalar multiplication, vector-matrix multiplication and other operations are redefined as Möbius operations and work in Riemannian manifolds with curvature \( c \) . These become the basic blocks of the hyperbolic neural networks. In particular, we will use the hyperbolic feed forward (FF) layer (also known as Möbius layer). Considering the Euclidean case, for a FF layer, we need a matrix \( \mathbf{M} : {\mathbb{R}}^{n} \rightarrow  {\mathbb{R}}^{m} \) to linearly project the input \( \mathbf{x} \in  {\mathbb{R}}^{n} \) to the feature space \( {\mathbb{R}}^{m} \) , and, additionally, a translation made by a bias addition, i.e., \( \mathbf{y} + \mathbf{b} \) with \( \mathbf{y},\mathbf{b} \in  {\mathbb{R}}^{m} \) and, finally, a pointwise non-linearity \( \phi  : {\mathbb{R}}^{m} \rightarrow  {\mathbb{R}}^{m}. \)

在双曲空间中推广张量运算的形式体系称为陀螺向量空间，其中加法、数乘、向量-矩阵乘法以及其他运算被重新定义为莫比乌斯运算，并在具有曲率 \( c \) 的黎曼流形上工作。这些构成双曲神经网络的基本模块。特别地，我们将使用双曲前馈(FF)层(也称为莫比乌斯层)。考虑欧几里得情形，对于一个 FF 层，我们需要一个矩阵 \( \mathbf{M} : {\mathbb{R}}^{n} \rightarrow  {\mathbb{R}}^{m} \) 将输入 \( \mathbf{x} \in  {\mathbb{R}}^{n} \) 线性投影到特征空间 \( {\mathbb{R}}^{m} \)；此外，还需要由偏置项(bias addition)带来的平移，即 \( \mathbf{y} + \mathbf{b} \) 与 \( \mathbf{y},\mathbf{b} \in  {\mathbb{R}}^{m} \)；最后还有一个逐点的非线性 \( \phi  : {\mathbb{R}}^{m} \rightarrow  {\mathbb{R}}^{m}. \)。

Matrix multiplication, bias and pointwise non-linearity are replaced by Möbius operations in the gyrovector space and become:

在陀螺向量空间中，矩阵乘法、偏置和逐点非线性分别被莫比乌斯运算取代，并变为:

\[
\mathbf{y} = {\mathbf{M}}^{{ \otimes  }_{c}}\left( \mathbf{x}\right)  = \frac{1}{\sqrt{c}}\tanh \left( {\frac{\parallel \mathbf{M}\mathbf{x}\parallel }{\parallel \mathbf{x}\parallel }{\tanh }^{-1}\left( {\sqrt{c}\parallel \mathbf{x}\parallel }\right) }\right) \frac{\mathbf{M}\mathbf{x}}{\parallel \mathbf{x}\parallel } \tag{3}
\]

\[
\mathbf{z} = \mathbf{y}{ \oplus  }_{c}\mathbf{b} = {\exp }_{\mathbf{y}}^{c}\left( {\frac{{\lambda }_{0}^{c}}{{\lambda }_{\mathbf{y}}^{c}}{\log }_{0}^{c}\left( \mathbf{b}\right) }\right) ,\;{\phi }^{{ \otimes  }_{c}}\left( \mathbf{z}\right)  = {\exp }_{\mathbf{z}}^{c}\left( {\phi \left( {{\log }_{0}^{c}\left( \mathbf{z}\right) }\right) }\right) \tag{4}
\]

where \( \mathbf{M} \) and \( \mathbf{b} \) are the same matrix and vector defined above, \( c \) is the magnitude of the curvature. Note that when \( c \rightarrow  0 \) we recover the Euclidean feed-forward layer. An interesting property of the Möbius layer is that it is highly nonlinear; indeed the bias addition in hyperbolic space becomes a nonlinear mapping since geodesics are curved paths in non-flat manifolds.

当 \( \mathbf{M} \) 和 \( \mathbf{b} \) 是上面定义的同一个矩阵与向量时，\( c \) 是曲率的大小。注意当 \( c \rightarrow  0 \) 时，我们恢复欧氏前馈层。莫比乌斯层的一个有趣性质是它高度非线性；事实上，在双曲空间中的偏置加法会变成一个非线性映射，因为测地线是在非平坦流形中弯曲的路径。

### 3.3 Hyperbolic Compositional Regularization

### 3.3 双曲组合式正则化

Armed with the formalism introduced in the previous section, we are ready to formulate our HyCoRe framework, anticipated in Fig. 1. Consider a point cloud \( {P}_{N} \) as a set of 3D points \( \mathbf{p} \in  {\mathbb{R}}^{3} \) with \( N \) elements. We use any state-of-the-art point cloud processing network as a feature extraction backbone \( E : {\mathbb{R}}^{N \times  3} \rightarrow  {\mathbb{R}}^{m} \) to encode \( {P}_{N} \) in the corresponding feature space. At this point we apply an exponential map \( {\exp }_{\mathbf{x}}^{c} : {\mathbb{R}}^{m} \rightarrow  {\mathbb{D}}^{m} \) to map the Euclidean feature vector into the hyperbolic space and then a Möbius layer \( H : {\mathbb{D}}^{m} \rightarrow  {\mathbb{D}}^{f} \) to project the hyperbolic vector in an \( f \) -dimensional Poincarè ball. This is the hyperbolic embedding of the whole point cloud \( {P}_{N} \) , i.e., \( {\mathbf{z}}_{\text{ whole }} = H\left( {\exp \left( {E\left( {P}_{N}\right) }\right) }\right)  \in  {\mathbb{D}}^{f} \) . We repeat the same procedure for a sub-part of \( {P}_{N} \) , which we call \( {P}_{{N}^{\prime }} \) with a number of points \( {N}^{\prime } < N \) , to create the part embedding \( {\mathbf{z}}_{\text{ part }} = H\left( {\exp \left( {E\left( {P}_{{N}^{\prime }}\right) }\right) }\right)  \in  {\mathbb{D}}^{f} \) in the same feature space as before.

借助上一节引入的形式主义，我们已经准备好提出我们的 HyCoRe 框架，如图 1 所示。将一个点云 \( {P}_{N} \) 视为由 \( N \) 个元素组成的一组 3D 点 \( \mathbf{p} \in  {\mathbb{R}}^{3} \)。我们使用任意最先进的点云处理网络作为特征提取骨干 \( E : {\mathbb{R}}^{N \times  3} \rightarrow  {\mathbb{R}}^{m} \)，将 \( {P}_{N} \) 编码到相应的特征空间中。此时，我们应用指数映射 \( {\exp }_{\mathbf{x}}^{c} : {\mathbb{R}}^{m} \rightarrow  {\mathbb{D}}^{m} \) 把欧氏特征向量映射到双曲空间，然后再使用莫比乌斯层 \( H : {\mathbb{D}}^{m} \rightarrow  {\mathbb{D}}^{f} \) 将双曲向量投影到一个 \( f \) 维的 Poincarè 球中。这就是整个点云 \( {P}_{N} \) 的双曲嵌入，即 \( {\mathbf{z}}_{\text{ whole }} = H\left( {\exp \left( {E\left( {P}_{N}\right) }\right) }\right)  \in  {\mathbb{D}}^{f} \) 。我们对 \( {P}_{N} \) 的一个子部分重复相同的过程——我们称之为 \( {P}_{{N}^{\prime }} \)，包含 \( {N}^{\prime } < N \) 个点——从而在与之前相同的特征空间中得到该部分嵌入 \( {\mathbf{z}}_{\text{ part }} = H\left( {\exp \left( {E\left( {P}_{{N}^{\prime }}\right) }\right) }\right)  \in  {\mathbb{D}}^{f} \)。

We now want to regularize the feature space to induce the previously mentioned properties, namely the part-whole hierarchy and clustering according to the class labels. This is performed by defining the following triplet regularizers:

接下来，我们希望对特征空间进行正则化，以诱导前面提到的性质，即基于类别标签的部分-整体层次结构和聚类。为此，我们定义以下三元组正则器:

\[
{R}_{\text{ hier }}\left( {{\mathbf{z}}_{\text{ whole }}^{ + },{\mathbf{z}}_{\text{ part }}^{ + }}\right)  = \max \left( {0, - {\begin{Vmatrix}{\mathbf{z}}_{\text{ whole }}^{ + }\end{Vmatrix}}_{\mathbb{D}} + {\begin{Vmatrix}{\mathbf{z}}_{\text{ part }}^{ + }\end{Vmatrix}}_{\mathbb{D}} + \gamma /{N}^{\prime }}\right) \tag{5}
\]

\[
{R}_{\text{ contr }}\left( {{\mathbf{z}}_{\text{ whole }}^{ + },{\mathbf{z}}_{\text{ part }}^{ + },{\mathbf{z}}_{\text{ part }}^{ - }}\right)  = \max \left( {0,{d}_{\mathbb{D}}\left( {{\mathbf{z}}_{\text{ whole }}^{ + },{\mathbf{z}}_{\text{ part }}^{ + }}\right)  - {d}_{\mathbb{D}}\left( {{\mathbf{z}}_{\text{ whole }}^{ + },{\mathbf{z}}_{\text{ part }}^{ - }}\right)  + \delta }\right) \tag{6}
\]

where \( {\mathbf{z}}_{\text{ whole }}^{ + } \) and \( {\mathbf{z}}_{\text{ part }}^{ + } \) are the hyperbolic representation of the whole and a part from the same point cloud, while \( {\mathbf{z}}_{\text{ part }}^{ - } \) is the embedding of a part of a different point cloud from a different class.

其中，\( {\mathbf{z}}_{\text{ whole }}^{ + } \) 和 \( {\mathbf{z}}_{\text{ part }}^{ + } \) 分别表示同一个点云中整体以及部分的双曲表示，而 \( {\mathbf{z}}_{\text{ part }}^{ - } \) 表示来自不同类别的另一点云中某个部分的嵌入。

The \( {R}_{\text{ hier }} \) regularizer in Eq. (5) induces the compositional part-whole hierarchy by promoting part embeddings to lie closer to the center of the Poincarè ball and whole embeddings to be closer to the edge. In particular, we use a variable margin \( \gamma /{N}^{\prime } \) that depends on the number of points \( {N}^{\prime } \) of the part \( {P}_{{N}^{\prime }} \) . This means that shapes composed by few points (hence simple universal shapes) will be far from the whole object representation and with lower hyperbolic norm (near the centre). On the other hand, em-beddings of larger parts will be progressively closer to the edge of the Poincarè ball, depending on the part size. Since geodesics between two points pass closer to the ball center (Fig. 3), this structure we impose to the space allows to visit common part ancestors while traversing a geodesic between two whole objects. This regularization thus mimics a continuous version of a part-whole tree embedded in the Poincarè ball.

式(5)中的 \( {R}_{\text{ hier }} \) 正则器通过将部分嵌入拉向 Poincarè 球的中心，并将整体嵌入拉向边缘，从而诱导组合式的部分-整体层次结构。具体而言，我们使用一个随部分点数 \( {N}^{\prime } \) 的变化而变化的可变裕度 \( \gamma /{N}^{\prime } \) ，对应部分 \( {P}_{{N}^{\prime }} \) 。这意味着由少量点构成的形状(因此是简单的通用形状)会远离整体的表示，并具有更低的双曲范数(靠近中心)。另一方面，更大部分的嵌入将会随着部分大小逐步更接近 Poincarè 球的边缘。由于连接任意两点的测地线会更靠近球心(图 3)，因此我们对该空间施加的这种结构使得在遍历两个整体对象之间的测地线时，可以访问共同的部分祖先。这样的正则化因此模仿了嵌入在 Poincarè 球中的部分-整体树的连续版本。

图 3:测地线路径。

The \( {R}_{\text{ contr }} \) regularizer in Eq. (6) promotes correct clustering of objects and parts in the hyperbolic space. In particular, parts and whole of the same point cloud are promoted to be close while a part from a different class is mapped far apart with respect to the other whole. It ensures that the parts of a point cloud of a different class are far in terms of geodesic distance. \( \delta \) is a margin hyperparameter to control the degree of separation between positive and negative samples.

式(6)中的 \( {R}_{\text{ contr }} \) 正则项有助于在双曲空间中实现物体与部件的正确聚类。具体而言，同一个点云中的部件与整体被拉近，而来自不同类别的部件与其他整体之间被映射到更远的位置。它确保不同类别点云的部件在测地距离意义下是远的。\( \delta \) 是一个边界超参数，用于控制正样本与负样本之间的分离程度。

The two regularizations are included in the final loss in this way:

这两种正则项以如下方式被纳入最终损失:

\[
L = {L}_{\mathrm{{CE}}} + \alpha {R}_{\text{ contr }} + \beta {R}_{\text{ hier }} \tag{7}
\]

where \( {L}_{\mathrm{{CE}}} \) is the conventional classification loss (e.g., cross-entropy) evaluated on the whole objects. The classification head is a hyperbolic Möbius layer followed by softmax. In principle, one could argue that \( {L}_{\mathrm{{CE}}} \) could already promote correct clustering according to class labels, rendering \( {R}_{\text{ contr }} \) redundant. However, several works [10] have noticed that the Möbius-softmax hyperbolic head is weaker than its Euclidean counterpart. We thus found it more effective to evaluate \( {L}_{\mathrm{{CE}}} \) on the whole objects only, and use \( {R}_{\text{ contr }} \) as a metric penalty that explicitly considers geodesic distances to ensure correct clustering of both parts and whole objects.

其中 \( {L}_{\mathrm{{CE}}} \) 是传统的分类损失(例如交叉熵)，在整体物体上进行评估。分类头是一个双曲 Möbius 层，后接 softmax。原则上，可以认为 \( {L}_{\mathrm{{CE}}} \) 已能根据类别标签促进正确聚类，从而使 \( {R}_{\text{ contr }} \) 变得多余。然而，已有多项工作 [10] 指出，Möbius-softmax 的双曲头比其欧式对应物更弱。因此，我们发现仅在整体物体上评估 \( {L}_{\mathrm{{CE}}} \) 更有效，并将 \( {R}_{\text{ contr }} \) 用作度量惩罚:它显式考虑测地距离，以确保部件与整体物体都能得到正确聚类。

At each iteration of training with HyCoRe we sample shapes with a random \( {N}^{\prime } \) varying within a predefined range. A part is defined as the \( {N}^{\prime } \) nearest neighbors of a random point. In future work, it would be interesting to explore alternative definitions for parts, e.g., using part labels if available but, at the moment, we only address definition via spatial neighbors to avoid extra labeling requirements.

在使用 HyCoRe 进行训练的每次迭代中，我们会采样形状，并让随机 \( {N}^{\prime } \) 在预设范围内变化。部件被定义为:随机一点的 \( {N}^{\prime } \) 个最近邻。在未来工作中，探索部件的替代定义会很有意思，例如在可用时使用部件标签，但目前我们只通过空间邻近来给出定义，以避免额外的标注需求。

### 4.1 Experimental setting

### 4.1 实验设置

We study the performance of our regularizer HyCoRe on the synthetic dataset ModelNet40 [27] (12,331 objects with 1024 points, 40 classes) and on the real dataset ScanObjectNN [28] (15,000 objects with 1024 points, 15 classes). We apply our method over multiple classification architectures, namely the widely popular DGCNN and PointNet++ baselines, as well as the recent state-of-the-art PointMLP model. We substitute the standard classifier with its hyperbolic version (Möbius+softmax), as shown in Fig. 1. We use \( f = {256} \) features to be comparable to the official implementations in the Euclidean space, then we test the model over different embedding dimensions in the ablation study. Moreover, we set \( \alpha  = \beta  = {0.01},\gamma  = {1000} \) and \( \delta  = 4 \) . For the number of points of each part \( {N}^{\prime } \) , we select a random number between 200 and 600, and for the whole object a random number between 800 and 1024 to ensure better flexibility of the learned to model to part sizes. We train the models using Riemannian SGD optimization. Our implementation is on Pytorch and we use geoopt [29] for the hyperbolic operations. Models are trained on an Nvidia A6000 GPU.

我们研究正则项 HyCoRe 在合成数据集 ModelNet40 [27](12,331 个物体，每个物体 1024 个点，40 个类别)以及真实数据集 ScanObjectNN [28](15,000 个物体，每个物体 1024 个点，15 个类别)上的性能。我们在多种分类架构上应用我们的方法，即广受欢迎的 DGCNN 与 PointNet++ 基线，以及近期的最先进模型 PointMLP。我们将标准分类器替换为其双曲版本(Möbius+softmax)，如图 1 所示。我们使用 \( f = {256} \) 特征，以便与欧氏空间中的官方实现保持可比；随后在消融研究中测试不同的嵌入维度。此外，我们设置 \( \alpha  = \beta  = {0.01},\gamma  = {1000} \) 和 \( \delta  = 4 \) 。对于每个部分的点数 \( {N}^{\prime } \) ，我们在 200 到 600 之间随机选择；对于整个物体，我们在 800 到 1024 之间随机选择，以确保所学习模型对部分规模具备更好的灵活性。我们使用黎曼 SGD 优化来训练模型。我们的实现基于 Pytorch，并使用 geoopt [29] 进行双曲运算。模型在 Nvidia A6000 GPU 上训练。

## 5 Conclusions

## 5 结论

Although deep learning in the hyperbolic space is in its infancy, in this paper we showed how it can successfully capture the hierarchical nature of 3D point clouds, boosting the performance of state-of-the-art models for classification. Reasoning about the relations between objects and the parts that compose them leads not only to better results but also more robust and explainable models. In the future, it would be interesting to explore different ways of defining parts, not based on spatial nearest neighbors but rather on more semantic constructions. One important extension is to adapt HyCoRe to segmentation. Since segmentation aims to classify single points and the corresponding parts, contrary to classification, the parts embeddings should be placed on the boundary of the Poincarè Ball, where there is more space to correctly cluster them, and the whole objects (made by composition of parts) near the origin. We could exploit the label of the parts to this end or investigate unsupervised settings where the part hierarchy emerges naturally.

尽管在双曲空间中进行深度学习尚处于起步阶段，本文展示了它如何成功捕捉三维点云的层次结构，从而提升用于分类的最先进模型性能。对对象及其组成部分之间关系的推理，不仅带来更好的结果，也能得到更稳健、可解释的模型。未来，探索不同的部分定义方式将很有意思:不再基于空间最近邻，而是基于更具语义的构建。一个重要的扩展是将 HyCoRe 适配到分割任务。与分类不同，分割旨在对单个点及其对应部分进行分类；因此，部分嵌入应放置在 Poincaré 球的边界上，因为那里有更多空间来正确聚类它们，而由部分组合得到的整个对象则靠近原点。为此，我们可以利用部分标签，或研究无监督设置，使部分层次能够自然涌现。
