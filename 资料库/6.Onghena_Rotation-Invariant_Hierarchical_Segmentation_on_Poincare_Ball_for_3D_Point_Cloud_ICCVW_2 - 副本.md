## Abstract

## 摘要

Point clouds are a set of data points in space to represent the 3D geometry of objects. A fundamental step in the processing is to achieve segmentation of the point cloud at different levels of detail. Within this context, hierarchical clustering (HC) breaks the point cloud down into coherent subsets to recognize the parts that make up the object. Along with classic approaches that build a hierarchical tree bottom-up using linkage criteria, recent developments exploit the tree-likeness of hyperbolic metric space, embedding data into the Poincaré Ball and capturing a hierarchical structure with low distortion. The main advantage of this kind of solution is the possibility to explore the space of discrete binary trees using continuous optimization. However, in this framework, a similarity function between points is assumed to be known, while this cannot always be granted for point cloud applications. In our method, we propose to use metric learning to fit at the same time the good similarity function and the optimal embedding into the hyperbolic space. Furthermore, when arbitrary rotations are applied to a 3D object, the pose should not influence the segmentation quality. Therefore, to avoid extensive data augmentation, we impose rotation invariance to ensure the uniqueness of the hierarchical segmentation of point clouds. We show the performance of our method on two datasets, ShapeNet and PartNet, at different levels of granularity. The results obtained are promising when compared to state-of-the-art flat segmentation. ${}^{1}$

点云是在空间中的一组数据点，用于表示物体的三维几何。处理的一个基础步骤是实现不同细节层次的分割。在此背景下，层次聚类(HC)将点云划分为连贯子集，以识别构成物体的部件。除利用连接准则自底向上构建层次树的经典方法外，近期工作利用双曲度量空间的树状特性，将数据嵌入庞加莱球并以低失真捕捉层次结构。这类方法的主要优点是可通过连续优化来探索离散二叉树的空间。然而，该框架通常假定点间相似性函数已知，而在点云应用中这并不总能保证。我们的方法提出同时使用度量学习来拟合合适的相似性函数和在双曲空间中的最优嵌入。此外，当对三维物体施加任意旋转时，姿态不应影响分割质量。因此，为避免大量数据增强，我们施加旋转不变性以保证点云层次分割的唯一性。我们在不同粒度层次的两个数据集 ShapeNet 和 PartNet 上展示方法性能。与最先进的平面分割相比，所获结果令人鼓舞。 ${}^{1}$

## 1. Introduction

## 1. 引言

In today's Information Age, digital data has become a commodity that is easily accessible and widely disseminated. As a response, many sectors in the industry have embraced new information technologies, including LiDAR technology, to facilitate and improve decision-making processes. Nowadays, it's common to create a digital twin or point cloud by scanning a 3D object with LiDAR. A crucial step is the semantic segmentation of a point cloud, which aims to partition each point with a label. It allows achieving an understanding of 3D objects or scenes in many applications such as infrastructure maintenance or autonomous vehicles. To allow an extensive and accurate analysis, deep learning enables us to improve the performance in the quality of predictions. However, a flat segmentation that creates a partition of nonoverlapping segments is not always sufficient to describe the input data.

在当今的信息时代，数字数据已成为一种易于获取和广泛传播的商品。为此，许多行业采用了包括 LiDAR 技术在内的新信息技术，以便促进和改进决策过程。如今，通过 LiDAR 扫描 3D 物体来创建数字孪生或点云已很常见。语义分割是关键步骤，旨在为每个点分配一个标签，使得在基础设施维护或自动驾驶等多种应用中能够理解 3D 对象或场景。为了进行广泛且精确的分析，深度学习可以提升预测质量的表现。然而，将输入划分为不重叠片段的平面分割并不总能充分描述输入数据。

Hierarchical Clustering (HC) provides an analysis of point clouds to improve the understanding of how parts are connected at different levels of detail. Hence, in contrast to flat segmentation, hierarchical clustering provides a hierarchy that structures parts across granularity levels, from coarse- to fine-grained concepts. Hierarchical clustering can be performed by a variety of techniques, including traditional algorithms based on similarities between points. These similarity-based HC methods leverage the pairwise similarities to decode a tree-like structure as the dendrogram with a linkage method. In particular, an approach has been proposed by Dasgupta [7] which, given a weighted-graph defined on a dataset, introduces a cost function to evaluate different hierarchical trees. However, the discrete optimization in this setting to find the optimal hierarchy is NP-Hard. Therefore, Chami et al. [5] exploit the ability of hyperbolic space to implicitly encode a hierarchical structure and propose a continuous relaxation of the Dasgupta's cost function in the Poincaré space.

层次聚类(HC)对点云进行分析，以改善对部件在不同细节层次上如何连接的理解。因此，与平面分割不同，层次聚类提供了一个在粗到细粒度概念间构建部件结构的层次。层次聚类可通过多种技术实现，包括基于点间相似性的传统算法。这些基于相似性的 HC 方法利用成对相似性并通过连结方法将其解码为树状结构(树状图)。特别地，Dasgupta [7] 提出了一种方法:在给定数据集上定义加权图后，引入了一个代价函数来评估不同的层次树。然而，在此设置下寻找最优层次的离散优化是 NP-难的。因此，Chami 等人 [5] 利用双曲空间隐式编码层次结构的能力，提出了在庞加莱空间中对 Dasgupta 代价函数的连续松弛。

Although the knowledge of similarities is a general hypothesis for weighted graphs, there is not a straightforward definition of a similarity relationship for point clouds. For example, the Euclidean distance is not informative enough, as two distinct points could be close to each other when separating two parts in space. Accordingly, hierarchical segmentation of point clouds is confronted with the barrier of finding a similarity function on the input space. To this end, we make use of state-of-the-art of deep learning models for point cloud segmentation to extract features of point clouds. The main contribution of this paper is threefold:

尽管对加权图而言相似性的已知性是一般假设，但对点云并不存在直接的相似性定义。例如，欧氏距离信息量不足，因为在空间上分隔两个部件时，两个不同点可能彼此接近。因此，点云的层次分割面临在输入空间上找到相似性函数的难题。为此，我们利用点云分割的最先进深度学习模型来提取点云特征。本文的主要贡献有三点:

1. We integrate metric learning in hyperbolic space to find a similarity function. At the same time, the similarity function is evaluated to determine an optimal embedding successfully.

1. 我们在双曲空间中整合度量学习以寻找相似性函数，同时评估该相似性函数以确定一个成功的最优嵌入。

## 2. Related Work

## 2. Related Work

Many research efforts in the field of hierarchical clustering have focused on the development of deep learning methods by offering a means of continuous representation. This optimization framework has enabled us to determine a hierarchy in hyperbolic space and support the functionality of point cloud segmentation that is invariant to its pose.

在层次聚类领域，许多研究致力于通过深度学习方法建立连续表示的手段。该优化框架使我们能够在双曲空间中确定层次结构，并支持对姿态不变的点云分割功能。

3D Point Cloud Processing The state-of-the-art application of deep learning to point cloud data is designed to process raw point cloud data [11]. PointNet [23] pioneered this manipulation, proposing to learn a stack of layers invariant by permutation, but is limited due to its inability to capture fine-grained concepts. Later, PointNet++ [24] introduced a hierarchical neural network to enhance the analysis of small neighborhoods by offering some notion of local similarity. Because of its inherent ambiguity in defining a neighborhood, over the years many ways of implementing convolution operations for point clouds have been proposed [17,29,34]. More recently, DGCNN [33] exploits the local neighborhood further by applying convolutions that compute edge features. VN-DGCNN [8] incorporates a framework in DGCNN to facilitate the construction of equivariance and invariance on arbitrary rotations.

3D 点云处理 将深度学习应用于点云数据的最先进做法旨在处理原始点云数据 [11]。PointNet [23] 首创此类处理，提出学习一组对置换不变的层，但因无法捕捉细粒度概念而受限。随后，PointNet++ [24] 引入了分层神经网络，通过引入局部相似性的概念来增强对小邻域的分析。由于定义邻域本身存在模糊性，近年来提出了多种针对点云实现卷积操作的方法 [17,29,34]。更近的工作 DGCNN [33] 通过应用计算边特征的卷积进一步利用局部邻域信息。VN-DGCNN [8] 在 DGCNN 中引入了一个框架，以便在任意旋转下构建等变性和不变性。

Hierarchical Clustering The development of Dasgupta [7] formulates a discrete optimization function for HC that guides a good hierarchy with low cost. Later, Wang and Wang [31] proposed an extended formulation to induce a consistent hierarchy given a similarity graph. Chami et al. [5] derive a continuous analogue in hyperbolic space to define a relaxation of Dasgupta's cost function. More importantly, they propose to learn an optimal embedding in the Poincaré disk, where a tree-like structure is observed to decode a hierarchy. In addition, as studied by Sarkar [26], hyperbolic space has the property to realize low distortion. The original approaches assume that the similarity structure between points is known in advance. In the work of Gigli et al. [10], the pairwise similarities were learned with the application of a triplet loss and the hyperbolic clustering function of Chami et al. [5]. While most works focus on applying a hyperbolic embedding in text or graphs, only limited research efforts have assessed the quality of hierarchical clustering for 3D point clouds. Nonetheless, other works use the hierarchical nature of the hyperbolic space along with metric learning to learn representation of images [9, 12]. In regard to 3D point clouds, Montanaro et al. [20] propose to embed point clouds to account the part-whole hierarchy for classification. Our segmentation contribution differs from [20] due to the use of a rotation-invariant framework presented in Section 3 and a large margin cosine loss in Section 4.

层次聚类 Dasgupta [7] 的工作提出了一个离散优化函数，为低代价的良好层次结构提供了指导。随后，Wang 和 Wang [31] 提出扩展形式以在给定相似性图时诱导一致的层次结构。Chami 等人 [5] 在双曲空间中推导出一个连续类比，定义了 Dasgupta 代价函数的松弛。更重要的是，他们提出学习在 Poincaré 圆盘中的最优嵌入，在那里可以观察到用于解码层次结构的树状结构。此外，正如 Sarkar [26] 所研究的，双曲空间具有实现低畸变的特性。最初的方法假设点之间的相似性结构事先已知。在 Gigli 等人 [10] 的工作中，通过三元组损失和 Chami 等人 [5] 的双曲聚类函数学习了成对相似性。尽管大多数工作侧重于在文本或图上应用双曲嵌入，但仅有有限研究评估了双曲层次聚类在 3D 点云上的质量。尽管如此，其他工作结合双曲空间的分层特性与度量学习来学习图像表示 [9,12]。关于 3D 点云，Montanaro 等人 [20] 提出将点云嵌入以考虑部件—整体层次用于分类。我们的分割工作不同于 [20]，因为采用了在第 3 节介绍的旋转不变框架以及在第 4 节的带大间隔余弦损失。

## 3. Background

## 3. 背景

To sharpen the intuition before defining our method, this section firstly reviews the background to introduce the optimization of hierarchical clustering in hyperbolic space.

为在定义方法前增强直观理解，本节首先回顾相关背景，以引入在双曲空间中进行层次聚类优化的方法。

### 3.1. Similarity-based HC

### 3.1. 基于相似性的层次聚类

The classical clustering procedure considers a dataset $\mathcal{D}$ of $n$ datapoints where the pairwise similarities ${\left( {w}_{ij}\right) }_{i, j \in  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  }$ are known in advance. The similarity between datapoints is employed by hierarchical clustering (HC) to construct a binary tree $\mathcal{T}$ where each leaf node corresponds to exactly one datapoint [13]. Clusters of different levels are formed by merging subtrees at internal nodes, in which the root node contains the entire dataset $\mathcal{D}$ . When two leaves $\left( {i, j}\right)$ are merged, their lowest common ancestor (LCA) is defined as $i \vee  j$ . Consequently, $\mathcal{T}\left\lbrack  {i \vee  j}\right\rbrack$ is the subtree that depicts the smallest cluster including both $i$ and $j$ . In the case of three leaf nodes $\left( {i, j, k}\right)$ , the relation $\{ i, j \mid  k\}$ holds if $i \vee  j$ occurs deeper in $\mathcal{T}$ than $i \vee  j \vee  k$ . The goal of similarity-based HC is to seek a hierarchical clustering by leveraging the similarity structure between datapoints [6]. Therefore, Dasgupta [7] proposed a cost function to assess the quality of a hierarchy:

经典的聚类过程考虑一个包含 $n$ 个样本的数据集 $\mathcal{D}$，已知样本间两两相似度 ${\left( {w}_{ij}\right) }_{i, j \in  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  }$。层次聚类(HC)利用这些相似度构建一棵二叉树 $\mathcal{T}$，每个叶节点恰对应一个样本[13]。通过在内部节点合并子树形成不同层次的簇，根节点包含整个数据集 $\mathcal{D}$。当两个叶子 $\left( {i, j}\right)$ 被合并时，它们的最低公共祖先(LCA)定义为 $i \vee  j$。因此，$\mathcal{T}\left\lbrack  {i \vee  j}\right\rbrack$ 表示包含 $i$ 与 $j$ 的最小簇的子树。对于三个叶节点 $\left( {i, j, k}\right)$，若 $i \vee  j$ 在 $\mathcal{T}$ 中比 $i \vee  j \vee  k$ 更深，则成立关系 $\{ i, j \mid  k\}$。基于相似性的 HC 目标是利用样本间的相似结构寻找层次聚类[6]。因此，Dasgupta [7] 提出一个代价函数来评估层次结构的质量:

$$
{C}_{\text{ Dasgupta }}\left( \mathcal{T}\right)  = \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{ij}}{w}_{ij}\left| {\operatorname{leaves}\left( {\mathcal{T}\left\lbrack  {i \vee  j}\right\rbrack  }\right) }\right| . \tag{1}
$$

In order to obtain a low cost, a high ${w}_{ij}$ should be cut as far down as possible in tree $\mathcal{T}$ to minimize the number of leaves in $\mathcal{T}\left\lbrack  {i \vee  j}\right\rbrack$ . Although the cost function (1) can initiate a good tree with a low cost, Wang and Wang [31] noticed that it resulted in an inconsistent hierarchy given a similarity structure. Therefore, they suggest a more meaningful cost function based on triplets of datapoints $i, j, k$ and their lowest common ancestors (LCA):

为了获得低代价，应该尽可能在树 $\mathcal{T}$ 中把高 ${w}_{ij}$ 向下切割，以最小化 $\mathcal{T}\left\lbrack  {i \vee  j}\right\rbrack$ 中的叶子数量。尽管代价函数 (1) 可产生代价较低的良好树，Wang 和 Wang [31] 指出基于相似结构它可能导致不一致的层次结构。因此，他们基于样本三元组 $i, j, k$ 及其最低公共祖先(LCA)提出了更有意义的代价函数:

$$
{C}_{\text{ Wang }}\left( {\mathcal{T};w}\right)  = \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{ijk}}\left\lbrack  {{w}_{ij} + {w}_{ik} + {w}_{jk} - {w}_{ijk}\left( {\mathcal{T};w}\right) }\right\rbrack   + 2\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{ij}}{w}_{ij},
$$

(2)

where

其中

$$
{w}_{ijk}\left( {\mathcal{T};w}\right)  = {w}_{ij}\mathbb{1}\left\lbrack  {\{ i, j \mid  k\} }\right\rbrack   + {w}_{ik}\mathbb{1}\left\lbrack  {\{ i, k \mid  j\} }\right\rbrack   + {w}_{jk}\mathbb{1}\left\lbrack  {\{ j, k \mid  i\} }\right\rbrack  .
$$

Consider a triplet $\left( {i, j, k}\right)$ and assume that ${w}_{ij}$ involves the largest similarity among the three pairwise similarities. The intuition implies that nodes $i$ and $j$ should be merged first before node $k$ to pursue a relation $\{ i, j \mid  k\}$ . The cost function is designed to reward this relation in $\mathcal{T}$ as it invokes the subtraction of the largest similarity ${w}_{ij}$ in the first term of equation (2). Consequently, the lower similarities ${w}_{ik}$ and ${w}_{jk}$ remain to minimize the loss. For a bad tree $\mathcal{T}$ that merges $k$ first with relation $\{ i, k \mid  j\}$ or $\{ j, k \mid  i\} ,{w}_{ij}$ is added only once and thus differs from Dasgupta's cost function. A HC is optimal when for all triplets, their relations in $\mathcal{T}$ are consistent with their similarities. Moreover, finding a tree that optimizes the cost function in (1) or (2) is NP-hard. Therefore, to introduce continuous optimization, Chami et al. [5] propose to relax (2) in the hyperbolic space.

考虑一个三元组 $\left( {i, j, k}\right)$，并假设 ${w}_{ij}$ 在三对相似度中最大。直觉上应先合并节点 $i$ 与 $j$，再合并节点 $k$，以追求关系 $\{ i, j \mid  k\}$。代价函数在 $\mathcal{T}$ 中通过在等式 (2) 的第一项减去最大相似度 ${w}_{ij}$ 来奖励这种关系。因此，较低的相似度 ${w}_{ik}$ 与 ${w}_{jk}$ 会留待最小化损失。对于一个将 $k$ 先合并且关系为 $\{ i, k \mid  j\}$ 或 $\{ j, k \mid  i\} ,{w}_{ij}$ 的糟糕树 $\mathcal{T}$，该项只被添加一次，因而不同于 Dasgupta 的代价函数。当所有三元组在 $\mathcal{T}$ 中的关系与它们的相似度一致时，HC 即为最优。此外，优化 (1) 或 (2) 中的代价函数以求得树是 NP-难的。因此，为引入连续优化，Chami 等人[5] 建议在双曲空间中放宽 (2)。

### 3.2. Hyperbolic HC

### 3.2. Hyperbolic HC

Hyperbolic geometry has shown great advantages in encoding complex information such as point clouds [20], or images [2,14]. In this section we review the elements necessary to understand our method. Keen readers can find more details at $\left\lbrack  {1,4,{25}}\right\rbrack$ .

双曲几何在编码点云 [20] 或图像 [2,14] 等复杂信息方面显示出巨大优势。本节回顾理解我们方法所需的要点。感兴趣的读者可在 $\left\lbrack  {1,4,{25}}\right\rbrack$ 查阅更多细节。

Hyperbolic Geometry A Riemannian manifold is defined as a pair consisting of a manifold $\mathcal{M}$ and a positive-definite inner product ${g}_{x}$ in the tangent space ${T}_{x}\mathcal{M}$ at each point $x \in  \mathcal{M}$ . One can define an exponential map ${\exp }_{x}$ which projects any vector $v$ of the tangent space ${T}_{x}\mathcal{M}$ onto $\mathcal{M}$ , such that ${\exp }_{x}\left( v\right)  \in  \mathcal{M}$ , and inversely a logarithmic map which projects any point in $\mathcal{M}$ back onto the tangent space at $x$ . The $N$ -dimensional hyperbolic space is an $N$ -dimensional Riemannian manifold of constant negative curvature $- c$ . It can be described using several isometric models [4]. We will use the Poincaré ball model $\left( {{\mathbb{B}}_{c}^{N},{g}_{c}^{\mathbb{B}}}\right)$ where ${\mathbb{B}}_{c}^{N} \mathrel{\text{ := }} \; \left\{  {p \in  {\mathbb{R}}^{N}\left| \right| c\parallel p{\parallel }^{2} \leq  1}\right\}$ . We assume $c > 0$ , such that ${\mathbb{B}}_{c}^{N}$ corresponds to a ball of radius $1/\sqrt{c}$ in Euclidean space. The Poincaré ball is coupled with a Riemannian metric ${g}_{c}^{\mathbb{B}}\left( p\right)  = \; {\left( \frac{2}{1 - c\parallel p\parallel }\right) }^{2}{g}_{\mathbb{E}}$ where $p \in  {\mathbb{B}}^{N}$ and ${g}_{\mathbb{E}}$ is the canonical metric of the Euclidean space. The geodesic distance between two points $p, q \in  {\mathbb{B}}^{N}$ is specified as:

双曲几何 黎曼流形定义为一个由流形 $\mathcal{M}$ 和每一点 $x \in  \mathcal{M}$ 处的切空间 ${T}_{x}\mathcal{M}$ 中的正定内积 ${g}_{x}$ 组成的对。可以定义一个指数映射 ${\exp }_{x}$，它将切空间 ${T}_{x}\mathcal{M}$ 中的任何向量 $v$ 投影到 $\mathcal{M}$ 上，使得 ${\exp }_{x}\left( v\right)  \in  \mathcal{M}$，反之，还有一个对数映射，它将 $\mathcal{M}$ 中的任何点投影回 $x$ 处的切空间。$N$ 维双曲空间是一个具有恒定负曲率 $- c$ 的 $N$ 维黎曼流形。它可以用几种等距模型来描述 [4]。我们将使用庞加莱球模型 $\left( {{\mathbb{B}}_{c}^{N},{g}_{c}^{\mathbb{B}}}\right)$，其中 ${\mathbb{B}}_{c}^{N} \mathrel{\text{ := }} \; \left\{  {p \in  {\mathbb{R}}^{N}\left| \right| c\parallel p{\parallel }^{2} \leq  1}\right\}$。我们假设 $c > 0$，使得 ${\mathbb{B}}_{c}^{N}$ 对应于欧几里得空间中半径为 $1/\sqrt{c}$ 的球。庞加莱球与一个黎曼度量 ${g}_{c}^{\mathbb{B}}\left( p\right)  = \; {\left( \frac{2}{1 - c\parallel p\parallel }\right) }^{2}{g}_{\mathbb{E}}$ 相关联，其中 $p \in  {\mathbb{B}}^{N}$ 且 ${g}_{\mathbb{E}}$ 是欧几里得空间的标准度量。两点 $p, q \in  {\mathbb{B}}^{N}$ 之间的测地距离规定为:

$$
{d}_{{\mathbb{B}}^{N}}\left( {p, q}\right)  = {\cosh }^{-1}\left( {1 + 2\frac{\parallel p - q{\parallel }^{2}}{\left( {1 - \parallel p{\parallel }^{2}}\right) \left( {1 - \parallel q{\parallel }^{2}}\right) }}\right) . \tag{3}
$$

With the origin $q = 0$ ,(3) could be simplified to:

当以原点 $q = 0$ 时，(3) 可简化为:

$$
{d}_{o}\left( p\right)  = d\left( {o, p}\right)  = 2{\tanh }^{-1}\left( {\parallel p\parallel }\right) . \tag{4}
$$

As opposed to Euclidean geometry, this particular space has the property to infer the underlying hierarchical structure of a dataset in its embedding $\left\lbrack  {2,{15}}\right\rbrack$ . Moreover, as researched by Nickel et al. [22], (3) is able to reflect the similarity of objects with their hyperbolic distance. In addition, a Poincaré embedding provides low distortion to simultaneously learn the hierarchy and similarities for large trees.

与欧几里得几何不同，该空间能够在其嵌入 $\left\lbrack  {2,{15}}\right\rbrack$ 中推断数据集的潜在层次结构。此外，正如 Nickel 等人 [22] 所研究的，(3) 能够用双曲距离反映对象间的相似性。另外，庞加莱嵌入在同时学习大树的层次结构与相似性时提供了低失真。

Hyperbolic Optimization The perspective of hyperbolic space to represent the similarity information between points as their hyperbolic distance will guide the learning of an underlying hierarchical structure. A differentiable function that enables the similarities is sought to arrange the embedded vertices. To propose a continuous version of Dasgupta's cost function (1), Chami et al. [5] leverage the hyperbolic properties to determine a respective LCA. Firstly, given two leaf nodes $i$ and $j$ of tree $\mathcal{T}$ , their LCA $i \vee  j$ is the closest node to the root $r$ that lies on the shortest path ${\pi }_{ij}$ connecting both $i$ and $j$ . More formally, $i \vee  j = \arg \mathop{\min }\limits_{{k \in  {\pi }_{ij}}}{d}_{\mathcal{T}}\left( {r, k}\right)$ measures the length of the path from the root $r$ of $\mathcal{T}$ to node $k$ . The same definition applies to the hyperbolic LCA, where the shortest path between two points ${z}_{i}$ and ${z}_{j}$ is represented by their geodesic and ${z}_{i} \vee  {z}_{j}$ is thus the node on that geodesic ${z}_{i} \rightsquigarrow  {z}_{j}$ closest to the origin (see Figure 1). Consequently, the hyperbolic LCA connects the two points in ${z}_{i} \vee  {z}_{j} \mathrel{\text{ := }} \arg \mathop{\min }\limits_{{z \in  {z}_{i} \rightsquigarrow  {z}_{j}}}{d}_{o}\left( z\right)$ and the hyperbolic distance between the origin and LCA is computed using (4).

### 3.3. Rotation-invariant Framework

### 3.3. 旋转不变框架

When arbitrary rotations are applied to a 3D object, the global object pose should not influence the segmentation quality. The most commonly used solution is to take advantage of data augmentation, where the inputs are randomly rotated during training to induce an insensible output to that transformation [16]. However, data augmentation does not imply neither equivariance nor invariance. There is a need to impose invariance while avoiding an extensive data augmentation of possible rotations during training time. A group-inspired solution [3], is to construct an invariant function, from a non-invariant function by summing over all the group SO(3), which is possible for image but intractable for 3D points. Another alternative is to use the fact that any feature computed on a Gram matrix is rotation invariant [27], however, this is computationally infeasible because an $n \times  n$ matrix should be stored in memory during training. Therefore, Deng et al. [8] propose a lightweight framework that is integrable into existing architectures as DGCNN. The main component is a Vector Neuron (VN) representation that lifts classical neurons with 3D vectors. A neural network consists out of neurons where each one takes a scalar input $x \in  \mathbb{R}$ . The stacked scalars form a $C$ dimensional latent feature that is fed to the neurons of a hidden layer. In specific, a classical feature $x = {\left\lbrack  {x}_{1},{x}_{2},\ldots ,{x}_{C}\right\rbrack  }^{\top } \in  {\mathbb{R}}^{C}$ with ${x}_{i} \in  \mathbb{R}$ is extended to a vector feature $V = {\left\lbrack  {v}_{1},{v}_{2},\ldots ,{v}_{C}\right\rbrack  }^{\top } \in  {\mathbb{R}}^{C \times  3}$ with ${v}_{i} \in  {\mathbb{R}}^{3}$ . Thus a point cloud with $n$ points, a collection $\mathcal{V} = \left\{  {{V}_{1},{V}_{2},\ldots ,{V}_{n}}\right\}   \in  {\mathbb{R}}^{n \times  C \times  3}$ is obtained where the change of latent channels $C$ follows a mapping between layers that only considers the second dimension of the tensor:

当任意旋转施加到 3D 物体上时，全局物体姿态不应影响分割质量。最常用的做法是利用数据增强，在训练期间对输入进行随机旋转以使输出对该变换不敏感 [16]。然而，数据增强既不等同于等变性也不等同于不变性。需要在避免在训练时对可能的旋转进行大量数据增强的同时强加不变性。一种受群论启发的解决方案 [3] 是通过对整个群 SO(3) 求和，从非不变函数构造不变函数，这在图像上可行但对 3D 点云不可行。另一种替代方法是利用任意在 Gram 矩阵上计算的特征都是旋转不变的事实 [27]，但这在计算上不可行，因为训练期间必须在内存中存储一个 $n \times  n$ 矩阵。因此，Deng 等人 [8] 提出了一种轻量级框架，可集成到现有架构如 DGCNN 中。其主要组件是将经典神经元提升为 3D 向量的向量神经元(VN)表示。神经网络由神经元组成，每个神经元接收一个标量输入 $x \in  \mathbb{R}$。堆叠的标量构成一个维度为 $C$ 的潜在特征，该特征被送入隐藏层的神经元。具体来说，带有 ${x}_{i} \in  \mathbb{R}$ 的经典特征 $x = {\left\lbrack  {x}_{1},{x}_{2},\ldots ,{x}_{C}\right\rbrack  }^{\top } \in  {\mathbb{R}}^{C}$ 被扩展为带有 ${v}_{i} \in  {\mathbb{R}}^{3}$ 的向量特征 $V = {\left\lbrack  {v}_{1},{v}_{2},\ldots ,{v}_{C}\right\rbrack  }^{\top } \in  {\mathbb{R}}^{C \times  3}$。因此，对于具有 $n$ 个点的点云，可得到一个集合 $\mathcal{V} = \left\{  {{V}_{1},{V}_{2},\ldots ,{V}_{n}}\right\}   \in  {\mathbb{R}}^{n \times  C \times  3}$，其中潜在通道数 $C$ 的变化遵循层间只考虑张量第二维的映射:

$$
{\mathcal{V}}^{\left( d + 1\right) } = f\left( {{\mathcal{V}}^{\left( d\right) };\theta }\right)  : {\mathbb{R}}^{n \times  {C}^{\left( d\right) } \times  3} \rightarrow  {\mathbb{R}}^{n \times  {C}^{\left( d + 1\right) } \times  3} \tag{6}
$$

with $d$ the layer depth and $\theta$ the learnable parameters of the network. The mapping (6) is required to induce rotation equivariance, i.e. for any rotation matrix $R \in \; \mathrm{{SO}}\left( 3\right) ,\;f\left( {\mathcal{V}R;\theta }\right)  = f\left( {\mathcal{V};\theta }\right) R$ . For more detailed information about the VN implementation of hidden layers - linear, non-linear, max pooling and normalization - we refer to the original paper of Deng et al. [8]. Nonetheless, the idea behind their invariant layer will be outlined as this is the property we seek within our method. Therefore, the invariant architecture leverages the output of equivariant VN layers to identify an object that is invariant to its pose. The basic principle is that the product of an equivariant signal $V \in  {\mathbb{R}}^{C \times  3}$ by the transpose of another equivariant signal $T \in  {\mathbb{R}}^{{C}^{\prime } \times  3}$ is rotation invariant, i.e., $\left( {VR}\right) {\left( TR\right) }^{\top } = {VR}{R}^{\top }{T}^{\top } = V{T}^{\top }$ for all rotation matrix $R$ . Note that as $V{T}^{\top }$ is a $C \times  {C}^{\prime }$ matrix, the computation is feasible for small values of ${C}^{\prime }$ . Accordingly, we consider ${C}^{\prime } = 3$ , and use the equivari-ant MLP proposed in [8], $T \mathrel{\text{ := }} \operatorname{VN-MLP}\left( \left\lbrack  {V,\bar{V}}\right\rbrack  \right)$ where $\bar{V} \mathrel{\text{ := }} \frac{1}{n}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{n}V \in  {\mathbb{R}}^{C \times  3}.$

其中 $d$ 为层深度，$\theta$ 为网络的可学习参数。映射 (6) 需诱导旋转等变，即对任意旋转矩阵 $R \in \; \mathrm{{SO}}\left( 3\right) ,\;f\left( {\mathcal{V}R;\theta }\right)  = f\left( {\mathcal{V};\theta }\right) R$ 均成立。关于 VN 隐藏层(线性、非线性、最大池化与归一化)的实现细节，详见 Deng 等人 [8] 的原文。尽管如此，我们仍将概述其不变层的核心思想，因为这正是我们方法所追求的特性。因此，该不变架构利用等变 VN 层的输出来识别与物体姿态无关的对象。基本原理是，等变信号 $V \in  {\mathbb{R}}^{C \times  3}$ 与另一等变信号 $T \in  {\mathbb{R}}^{{C}^{\prime } \times  3}$ 的转置相乘是旋转不变的，即对所有旋转矩阵 $R$ 均有 $\left( {VR}\right) {\left( TR\right) }^{\top } = {VR}{R}^{\top }{T}^{\top } = V{T}^{\top }$。注意由于 $V{T}^{\top }$ 是一个 $C \times  {C}^{\prime }$ 矩阵，当 ${C}^{\prime }$ 较小时计算是可行的。据此，我们考虑 ${C}^{\prime } = 3$，并采用 [8] 中提出的等变 MLP $T \mathrel{\text{ := }} \operatorname{VN-MLP}\left( \left\lbrack  {V,\bar{V}}\right\rbrack  \right)$，其 $\bar{V} \mathrel{\text{ := }} \frac{1}{n}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{n}V \in  {\mathbb{R}}^{C \times  3}.$

## 4. Method

## 4. 方法

The goal of Chami et al. [5] was to discover a hierarchy of datapoints by leveraging their given pairwise similarities. In this work, the similarities are unknown but learned end-to-end together with the optimization of a hyperbolic embedding. Therefore, a similarity function is learned such that it would enable us to determine a hierarchy in a Poincaré ball ${\mathbb{B}}^{N}$ . The objective to seek a good similarity function is defined by the Large Margin Cosine Loss (LMCL) [32] with margin $m$ and scale $s$ :

Chami 等人 [5] 的目标是利用已给定的两两相似度发现数据点的层级。在本工作中，相似度未知，而是与双曲嵌入的端到端优化一同学习。因此学习一个相似度函数，使我们能够在庞加莱球 ${\mathbb{B}}^{N}$ 中确定层级。寻求良好相似度函数的目标由带有边界 $m$ 和尺度 $s$ 的大间隔余弦损失(LMCL)[32] 定义:

$$
{\mathcal{L}}_{\mathrm{{LMCL}}}\left( {Z, A;m, s}\right)  =
$$

$$
\frac{1}{n}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{i} - \log \frac{{e}^{s\left( {{A}_{{y}_{i}}^{T}{z}_{i}^{ * } - m}\right) }}{{e}^{s\left( {{A}_{{y}_{i}}^{T}{z}_{i}^{ * } - m}\right) } + \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{j \neq  {y}_{i}}}{e}^{s{A}_{j}^{T}{z}_{i}^{ * }}}, \tag{7}
$$

subject to $\parallel A\parallel  = 1$ , where ${A}_{N \times  J}$ is a learnable matrix, $J$ the number of classes, and ${z}^{ * } \mathrel{\text{ := }} z/\parallel z\parallel$ . In (7) ${z}_{i}$ is the $i$ -th feature vector corresponding to the ground-truth class of ${y}_{i}$ , the ${A}_{j}$ is the weight vector of the $j$ -th class. Note that due to norm-one constraints ${A}_{j}^{T}{z}_{i}^{ * } = \cos \left( {\theta }_{ij}\right)$ where ${\theta }_{ij}$ the angle between ${A}_{j}$ and ${z}_{i}$ . Geometrically, it means that we assign the sample $i$ to class $j$ if the angle between ${A}_{j}$ and ${z}_{i}$ is the smallest among all class centers ${A}_{j}$ , and $m$ plays the role of angular margin in the decision.

在约束于 $\parallel A\parallel  = 1$ 的条件下，其中 ${A}_{N \times  J}$ 是可学习矩阵，$J$ 为类别数，且 ${z}^{ * } \mathrel{\text{ := }} z/\parallel z\parallel$ 。在 (7) 中，${z}_{i}$ 是对应于样本真实类别 ${y}_{i}$ 的第 $i$ 个特征向量，${A}_{j}$ 是第 $j$ 类的权重向量。注意由于范数为一的约束有 ${A}_{j}^{T}{z}_{i}^{ * } = \cos \left( {\theta }_{ij}\right)$，其中 ${\theta }_{ij}$ 为 ${A}_{j}$ 与 ${z}_{i}$ 之间的夹角。从几何上讲，这意味着当 ${A}_{j}$ 与 ${z}_{i}$ 之间的夹角在所有类中心 ${A}_{j}$ 中最小时，我们将样本 $i$ 归为类 $j$，而 $m$ 在判决中起到角度间隔的作用。

In this method, deep metric learning is integrated with the cost function in (5) to define similarities in hyperbolic space. As a consequence, the LMCL computed using cosine similarity is now evaluated in the Poincaré ball ${\mathbb{B}}^{N}$ to learn a similarity function ${w}_{i, j} = \frac{1}{2}\left( {1 - \frac{\left\langle  {z}_{i},{z}_{j}\right\rangle  }{\begin{Vmatrix}{z}_{i}\end{Vmatrix}\begin{Vmatrix}{z}_{j}\end{Vmatrix}}}\right)$ . By intuition, with the operation of both hyperbolic and LMCL loss in ${\mathbb{B}}^{N}$ , we think this would improve similarity learning and hyperbolic clustering. Hence, the loss function to find optimal parameters $\theta$ and optimal embedding $Z \in  {\mathbb{B}}^{N}$ is the following:

在此方法中，深度度量学习与 (5) 中的代价函数集成，以在双曲空间中定义相似度。因此，使用余弦相似度计算的 LMCL 现改为在庞加莱球 ${\mathbb{B}}^{N}$ 中评估，以学习相似度函数 ${w}_{i, j} = \frac{1}{2}\left( {1 - \frac{\left\langle  {z}_{i},{z}_{j}\right\rangle  }{\begin{Vmatrix}{z}_{i}\end{Vmatrix}\begin{Vmatrix}{z}_{j}\end{Vmatrix}}}\right)$。直观上，双曲操作与在 ${\mathbb{B}}^{N}$ 中的 LMCL 损失共同作用，会改进相似度学习与双曲聚类。因此，寻找最优参数 $\theta$ 与最优嵌入 $Z \in  {\mathbb{B}}^{N}$ 的损失函数如下:

$$
\mathop{\min }\limits_{{\parallel A\parallel  = 1, Z \in  \mathcal{Z}}}\lambda {C}_{\mathrm{{HypHC}}}\left( {Z, w;\tau }\right)  + {\mathcal{L}}_{\mathrm{{LMCL}}}\left( {Z, A;m, s}\right) , \tag{8}
$$

where $\lambda$ is a trade-off parameter to control the importance of the hyperbolic loss.

其中 $\lambda$ 是用于控制双曲损失重要性的权衡参数。

With the hyperbolic space ${\mathbb{B}}^{N}$ in mind, the provided point cloud object will first be represented by a neural network in the feature space to extract geometric properties. The learned embedding, established by grouping points in Euclidean space ${\mathbb{R}}^{N}$ , is projected to the Poincaré ball in ${\mathbb{B}}^{N}$ according to the Riemannian metric. At this point, the framework in figure 2 illustrates that the point cloud $X$ is embedded in feature space by a neural network namely VN-DGCNN. This feature embedding $U$ is in turn projected as a Poincaré embedding $Z$ to optimize the loss function defined in (8) and encode a hierarchical structure. With the point clouds $X$ and corresponding class labels $Y$ , we recognize a supervised learning framework where the labels $Y$ are leveraged for classification of LMCL and to define triplets in the computation of the hyperbolic loss. The challenge of estimating similarities and seeking an optimal hyperbolic embedding at the same time imposes the need for a more efficient triplet sampling technique. By defining a set of easy triplets ${\mathcal{T}}_{\text{ Hyp }}$ , we provide the hyperbolic loss with estimated similarities to first merge pairs of the same class. The approach is that LMCL triggers hard triplets to become easy. These easy triplets are in turn applied to the hyperbolic loss to perform hierarchical clustering.

考虑到双曲空间 ${\mathbb{B}}^{N}$，给定的点云对象首先由神经网络在特征空间中表示以提取几何属性。由在欧几里得空间中将点分组得到的学习嵌入 ${\mathbb{R}}^{N}$，根据黎曼度量投影到庞加莱球 ${\mathbb{B}}^{N}$。此时，图 2 所示框架表明点云 $X$ 由名为 VN-DGCNN 的神经网络嵌入到特征空间。该特征嵌入 $U$ 进而被投影为庞加莱嵌入 $Z$，以优化在 (8) 中定义的损失并编码层级结构。对于点云 $X$ 及相应类别标签 $Y$，我们识别出一个监督学习框架，其中标签 $Y$ 被用于 LMCL 的分类并在计算双曲损失时定义三元组。与此同时估计相似度并寻求最优双曲嵌入的挑战要求更高效的三元组采样技术。通过定义一组简单三元组 ${\mathcal{T}}_{\text{ Hyp }}$，我们为双曲损失提供估计相似度以先合并同类对。该方法使 LMCL 将困难三元组触发为简单三元组；这些简单三元组随即被用于双曲损失以执行层级聚类。

The objective is to produce an embedding that structures different parts in the Poincaré space. With the leaf nodes representing points of an object, we seek a leaf embedding that organizes connected points more close to each together than unrelated ones. This process of differentiating between points is subject to similarity learning by including LMCL. It induces the convergence of similar points in the Poincaré space to form parts at segmentation levels. Moreover, we aim to arrange a Poincaré space such that it's able to decode a binary tree that captures the hierarchical structure of an object. This goal is illustrated in figure 3, where each part is ordered in a way such that a merge with neighboring parts induces a higher level in the hierarchy.

目标是生成在庞加莱空间中能结构化不同部件的嵌入。以叶节点表示物体的点，我们希望叶嵌入将相互连接的点彼此靠得更近，而与之无关的点更远。通过引入 LMCL 进行相似性学习来实现点之间的区分，它促使相似点在庞加莱空间中聚拢以在分割层次上形成部件。此外，我们旨在组织一个能够解码出表示物体层次结构的二叉树的庞加莱空间。如图3 所示，每个部件按一种顺序排列，使得与相邻部件合并会在层次中产生更高一级。

Architecture The DGCNN architecture for part segmentation is applied with the VN network to provide robustness over rotations. Consequently, vector neurons are plugged in the DGCNN architecture to create VN-DGCNN that will be used to study the performance of our method. The DGCNN architecture corresponds to VN-DGCNN with integrated VN layers. Three EdgeConv layers are used to capture local geometric features. The information from these layers is aggregated to encode a latent space that is in turn decoded by a MLP network into a feature embedding. This transformation is accompanied by a categorical vector to decode the latent space in an oriented way according to the object category. In specific, when training a model for all categories, the categorical vector is defined by one-hot encoding to adjust the feature embedding to the category of the object running through the network.

架构 对零件分割采用 DGCNN 架构并引入 VN 网络以提高对旋转的鲁棒性。因此，在 DGCNN 中嵌入向量神经元，得到用于评估我们方法性能的 VN-DGCNN。DGCNN 架构对应于整合了 VN 层的 VN-DGCNN。使用三层 EdgeConv 捕获局部几何特征。来自这些层的信息被聚合以编码潜在空间，随后由 MLP 解码为特征嵌入。该变换伴随一个类别向量，用以根据对象类别有方向地解码潜在空间。具体地，当训练一个涵盖所有类别的模型时，类别向量以独热编码定义，以将特征嵌入调整到通过网络的对象类别。

Metric Let $k$ be the number of clusters that we want to determine in the generated dendrogram. For the evaluation of an object, we recursively iterate over $k$ to obtain a partition of $k$ clusters from the hierarchy. For each $k$ , the clustering quality of the prediction is measured by using the Intersection-over-Union (IoU) score. However, before computing the IoU score, the ground-truth labels $\left\{  {{l}_{1},\ldots ,{l}_{n}}\right\}$ are associated with the cluster prediction labels $\left\{  {{c}_{1},\ldots ,{c}_{k}}\right\}$ . Therefore, we compute the matrix that contains the IoU scores of all couples ${\left( {l}_{i},{c}_{j}\right) }_{i, j}$ between the classes and clusters. To each label in the ground truth, we assign the predicted label that maximizes the IoU score as follows, ${l}_{i} \leftarrow  \arg \mathop{\max }\limits_{{j \in  1,\ldots , k}}\operatorname{IoU}\left( {{l}_{i},{c}_{j}}\right)$ . Moreover, we introduce other to classify all the remaining prediction labels and penalize the prediction when the number of clusters $k$ becomes higher. The IoU score is then computed by using the remapped prediction labels. Finally, the $k$ of the hierarchy that gives the highest IoU in comparison to the ground truth is selected. To compare our method with other benchmarks, the mean IoU (mIoU) is calculated for a category at a segmentation level by averaging the IoUs of all test objects. Thus, we observe the performance with the use of shape mIoU.

度量 设 $k$ 为我们希望在生成的树状图中确定的簇数。为评估一个对象，我们对 $k$ 进行递归遍历，从层次结构中得到 $k$ 个簇的划分。对于每个 $k$，通过交并比(IoU)得分衡量预测的聚类质量。然而，在计算 IoU 之前，需将地面真值标签 $\left\{  {{l}_{1},\ldots ,{l}_{n}}\right\}$ 与簇的预测标签 $\left\{  {{c}_{1},\ldots ,{c}_{k}}\right\}$ 相关联。因此，我们构建包含类别与簇之间所有配对 ${\left( {l}_{i},{c}_{j}\right) }_{i, j}$ 的 IoU 分数的矩阵。对于地面真值中的每个标签，我们分配使 IoU 最大化的预测标签，如下所示，${l}_{i} \leftarrow  \arg \mathop{\max }\limits_{{j \in  1,\ldots , k}}\operatorname{IoU}\left( {{l}_{i},{c}_{j}}\right)$。此外，我们引入“其他”类别以归入所有剩余的预测标签，并在簇数 $k$ 增加时对预测进行惩罚。然后使用重映射后的预测标签计算 IoU 分数。最终，选择与地面真值比较时 IoU 最高的层次结构的 $k$。为将我们的方法与其他基准比较，按分割级别对类别的所有测试对象的 IoU 求平均，计算平均 IoU(mIoU)。由此可观察基于形状的 mIoU 性能。

Hyperparameters For the experiments, 1024 points are randomly sampled from the point cloud to train, validate and test the model. We select complete-linkage to decode the Poincaré ball into a corresponding dendrogram. In addition, it's noticed that the number of points differs for every class in a 3D object. Therefore, the number of triplets for an underrepresented class could be increased to an extent such that it would have more influence on the hyperbolic loss and be segmented.

我们选择完全链接 (complete-linkage) 将 Poincaré 球解码为对应的树状图。为了利用 LMCL 学到的相似性函数，该凝聚聚类方法采用余弦相似度作为度量。此外，注意到三维物体中每个类别的点数各不相同。因此，可以在一定程度上增加样本不足类别的三元组数量，使其对双曲损失有更大影响并被分割出来。

## 6. Conclusion

## 6. Conclusion

We applied metric learning to study the performance of hierarchical clustering in hyperbolic space. In particular for 3D point clouds, where a similarity function needs to be learned to arrange its parts over different levels of detail. We trained a rotation-invariant architecture on two datasets to examine our method of hierarchical segmentation and inspect any tradeoffs over flat segmentation. The proposed method achieves the best performance among the SO(3) group for ShapeNet and obtains very competitive hierarchical segmentation results trained on the finest level of PartNet. As future work, we can suggest the use of loss functions where the different levels of the hierarchy are specifically included as in $\left\lbrack  {{15},{21},{28}}\right\rbrack$ .

我们将度量学习应用于研究双曲空间中分层聚类的性能。针对 3D 点云，需学习相似性函数以在不同细节层次上组织其部件。我们在两个数据集上训练了一个旋转不变的架构，以验证我们的分层分割方法并比较其与平面(非分层)分割的权衡。所提方法在 ShapeNet 的 SO(3) 群中取得了最佳性能，并在 PartNet 的最细层级上训练时获得了极具竞争力的分层分割结果。作为未来工作，我们建议使用将层级的不同层级明确纳入的损失函数，如 $\left\lbrack  {{15},{21},{28}}\right\rbrack$ 中所示。
