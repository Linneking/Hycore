## ABSTRACT

## 摘要

Hyperbolic neural networks are emerging as an effective technique to better capture hierarchical representations of many data types, from text to images and, recently, point clouds. In this paper, we extend our earlier work, that showed how to use regularizers in the hyperbolic space to improve performance of point cloud classification models, to the problem of part segmentation. This requires careful modeling of the hierarchical relationships between parts and whole point cloud to properly control the hyperbolic geometry of the feature space produced by the neural network. We show how the proposed method improves the performance of commonly used neural network architectures, reaching state-of-the-art performance on the part segmentation task.

超曲率神经网络正成为更好地捕捉多种数据(从文本到图像，近来还包括点云)层级表示的有效方法。本文将我们早期在超曲率空间中使用正则化以提升点云分类模型性能的工作扩展到零件分割问题。为此需谨慎建模部分与整体点云之间的层级关系，以恰当控制神经网络生成的特征空间的超曲率几何。我们展示了所提方法如何提升常用网络架构的性能，并在零件分割任务上达到最先进的表现。

Index Terms- Point clouds, part segmentation, hyperbolic neural networks

索引项- 点云、零件分割、超曲率神经网络

## 1. INTRODUCTION

## 1. 引言

The compositional nature of 3D objects, for which the point cloud representation is one of the most commonly used, is evident in the fact that a whole object can be decomposed in progressively more elementary constituent parts. This induces a hierarchy where on one end simple universal shapes are found, whose combination leads to the other end where the complex geometries of individual objects are found. Mathematically, a tree can be used to represent such hierarchy where one can place a full object at root, whereas leaves are generated by progressively decomposing the object into the constituent parts.

3D 物体的组成性质在点云表示中尤为明显:整体可被逐步分解为更基本的组成部分。这产生了一个层级结构，一端是简单的通用形状，组合后生成另一端的个体复杂几何体。数学上可用树来表示该层级:将完整物体置于根处，叶子通过逐步分解得到各组成部分。

Deep learning models for point cloud data have become increasingly powerful in the last years, exploiting sophisticated building blocks such as graph neural networks [1] to overcome the irregularity of the point cloud domain. However, despite some attempts, an effective exploitation of the aforementioned hierarchical nature of data has remained elusive. Indeed, works like PointGRL [2], DCGLR [3], Info3D [4] recognized the importance of capturing the interactions between local and global features and while they provide good performance in unsupervised problems, they fail to model such dependencies in the supervised setting. In our recent work [5], we showed that this is due to the flawed geometry of the feature spaces adopted by these methods. Spaces that are either flat (Euclidean) or with positive curvature (spherical) are not able to embed tree structures representing hierarchies of features with low distortion. It is instead known that only spaces with negative curvature (hyperbolic) allow the exponential increase in volume as function of the radius that is necessary to represent trees. Our earlier work [5] focused on the point cloud classification problem, where it was shown that moving to the hyperbolic space and using regularizers to explicitly guide the geometry of the feature space to incorporate the part-whole hierarchy resulted in improved performance of any state-of-the-art neural network model for the problem.

近年来，针对点云数据的深度学习模型变得愈发强大，利用图神经网络等复杂构件来克服点云领域的不规则性。然而，尽管有若干尝试，前述数据的层级性仍未被有效利用。诸如 PointGRL [2]、DCGLR [3]、Info3D [4] 的工作认识到捕捉局部与全局特征相互作用的重要性，并在无监督问题上表现良好，但在有监督场景中未能建模此类依赖关系。在我们近期的工作 [5] 中，我们指出这是由于这些方法所采用的特征空间几何存在缺陷。平坦(欧氏)或正曲率(球面)的空间无法以低失真嵌入表示特征层级的树结构。而已知只有负曲率(双曲)空间才能使体积随半径呈指数增长，从而能表示树结构。我们早前的工作 [5] 聚焦于点云分类问题，证明迁移到双曲空间并使用正则化器显式引导特征空间几何以包含部件-整体层级，可提升任一最先进神经网络模型的性能。

In this paper, we address the point cloud part segmentation problem, expanding the previous work with a novel regularization framework called HyCoRe-seg (Hyperbolic Compositional Regularization for part segmentation). We will show that there are significant differences in the construction of effective hyperbolic regularizers between the whole point cloud classification problem and part segmentation. In fact, we will discuss how, somewhat counterintuitively, part segmentation requires one to enforce a part-whole hierarchy that is reversed with respect to the case of classification of whole objects. Moreover, we also introduce improvements to how parts are defined for purpose of regularization, showing that assembling them as clusters of points in a feature space increases effectiveness thanks to the exploitation of self-similarities.

本文研究点云部件分割问题，在此前工作的基础上提出一种新正则框架 HyCoRe-seg(用于部件分割的双曲组合正则化)。我们将展示，在为部件分割构建有效的双曲正则器时，与整体点云分类存在显著差异。事实上，我们将讨论一种有些反直觉的情况:部件分割需要强制的部件-整体层级与整体分类情况相反。此外，我们还改进了用于正则化的部件定义，表明将部件组装为特征空间中的点簇通过利用自相似性能提高有效性。

Overall, our main contributions can be summarized as:

总体而言，我们的主要贡献可归纳为:

- we propose a novel regularizer for supervised training of point cloud part segmentation models using neural networks;

- 我们提出了一种用于有监督训练的点云部件分割神经网络的新型正则器；

- this expands the previously known technique for classification, investigating some counterintuitive differences in the hierarchy design;

- 该方法扩展了先前用于分类的技术，探讨了层级设计中若干反直觉的差异；

- we show that defining parts as neighborhoods in a feature space rather than in the original 3D space improves regularization effectiveness;

- 我们证明将部件定义为特征空间中的邻域而非原始三维空间中的邻域，可以提高正则化的有效性；

- we experimentally show improvements on widely-used neural networks for part segmentation.

- 我们通过实验证明在广泛使用的部件分割神经网络上获得了性能提升。

## 2. BACKGROUND

## 2. 背景

### 2.1. Point cloud neural networks

### 2.1. 点云神经网络

Point clouds are unordered collections of positions in 3D space. This irregular domain makes them very challenging to process with neural networks which cannot exploit widely known primitives such as convolution. Recent years have seen progressive refinements of neural network architectures capable of working on the raw 3D coordinates, starting from PointNet [6], which processed each point independently before aggregating them with a globally symmetric operation, and PointNet++ [7], which introduced localized operations. Graph neural networks like DGCNN [8] define a graph to capture relations among points and successfully exploited graph convolutional operations to extract localized and hierarchical features.

点云是三维空间中无序的位置集合。这样的不规则域使得神经网络难以处理，因为无法利用诸如卷积等常用算子。近年来，能直接处理原始三维坐标的网络架构不断改进:从 PointNet [6] 开始，该方法先独立处理每个点，再用全局对称操作聚合它们；PointNet++ [7] 引入了局部化操作；像 DGCNN [8] 这样的图神经网络通过构建图来捕捉点间关系，并成功利用图卷积操作提取局部和分层特征。

## 3. PROPOSED METHOD

## 3. PROPOSED METHOD

An overview of the proposed method and how it fits a generic neural network architecture for the segmentation task is shown in Fig. 1. At a high level, the segmentation head of the neural network is replaced with a hyperbolic counterpart with Mobius layers. The segmentation loss is augmented by two regularizers which work on the embedding of the entire point cloud (feature vector average-pooled from all points) and of a part (feature vector average-pooled from a neighborhood of points, defined as $k$ -NN in feature space) after mapping to the hyperbolic space. The contrastive regularizer promotes separation among embeddings of different classes, while the hierarchy regularizer promotes a part-whole hierarchy, placing whole objects closer to the origin of the Poincarè ball and smaller parts towards the edge. As mentioned, the geometry of this hyperbolic setting is very different from that employed in [5] for classification; the geometry is further explained in Sec. 3.1, whereas intuition about the obtained whole-part hierarchy is given in Sec. .

如图 1 所示，给出所提出方法的概览及其如何适配用于分割任务的通用神经网络架构。总体上，神经网络的分割头被带有 Möbius 层的双曲对应模块所替代。分割损失通过两个正则项增强，这两个正则项作用于映射到双曲空间后的整个点云的嵌入(从所有点平均池化得到的特征向量)以及部分的嵌入(从点的邻域平均池化得到的特征向量，邻域在特征空间中定义为 $k$ -NN)。对比正则项促进不同类别嵌入间的分离，而层次正则项促进部分—整体层级关系，将整体物体置于庞加莱球的靠近原点位置、较小部分置于靠近边缘的位置。如前所述，这一双曲设定的几何性质与文献[5]中用于分类的几何大不相同；几何细节在 Sec. 3.1 中进一步解释，而关于所得整体—部分层级的直观说明见 Sec. 。

### 3.1. Hyperbolic feature space regularization

### 3.1. 超曲率特征空间正则化

In this section, we present the proposed method to regularize neural networks for part segmentation of point clouds via the hyperbolic space. Let the input to the model be a point cloud ${P}_{N}$ as a set of $N$ 3D points $\mathbf{p} \in  {\mathbb{R}}^{3}$ . We consider a generic encoder backbone providing a feature vector per point, i.e., $E : {\mathbb{R}}^{N \times  3} \rightarrow  {\mathbb{R}}^{N \times  F}$ . A segmentation head processes these features to derive class scores for each point. In HyCoRe-seg, this module is implemented with hyperbolic neural network layers. In particular, we use an exponential map to move from the Euclidean to hyperbolic space and then use one or more Mobius layers shared across points to reduce the dimensionality from $F$ to the desired number of classes.

在本节中，我们提出通过超曲率空间对点云部件分割神经网络进行正则化的方法。设模型的输入为点云 ${P}_{N}$，由 $N$ 个 3D 点 $\mathbf{p} \in  {\mathbb{R}}^{3}$ 组成。我们考虑一个通用的编码器骨干，为每个点提供一个特征向量，即 $E : {\mathbb{R}}^{N \times  3} \rightarrow  {\mathbb{R}}^{N \times  F}$。一个分割头处理这些特征以得到每个点的类别得分。在 HyCoRe-seg 中，该模块由超曲率神经网络层实现。具体地，我们使用指数映射从欧氏空间移动到超曲率空间，然后采用一个或多个在点间共享的 Möbius 层将维度从 $F$ 降至所需的类别数。

We seek to regularize the feature space produced by the encoder and ultimately used to estimate the segmentation labels so that it leverages prior knowledge about the existence of a hierarchy between parts and whole objects. In particular, we assume that this hierarchy can be described by a tree whose root is a whole object and leaves are small constituent parts. Each level of the hierarchy decomposes the object into smaller and simpler parts. This tree can be embedded in the Poincarè ball by placing the embedding of a whole object close to the origin of the ball and the embedding of small parts close to the ball edge. This is motivated by the properties of the hyperbolic space. In fact, a geodesic (shortest path) between two points passes closer to the origin, emulating the fact that traversing a tree from leaf to leaf requires to pass closer to the root. With this objective in mind, we need to analytically define what a part is for the purpose of training. A straightforward definition would be a number of spatially neighboring points, or the subset defined by the available part labels. Notice, however, that our definition of hierarchy may be more general than the specific semantic part labels that are available, so it even makes sense to create parts, for the purpose of regularization, that do not necessarily follow either the labels or clear semantic concepts. In this paper, we introduce a different definition of part, based on finding the ${N}^{\prime }$ nearest neighbors of a random point in the feature space produced by the encoder $E$ . This definition allows to capture "parts" in a more general sense and exploiting, possibly nonlocal, self-similarities thanks to the fact that neighbors in a feature space capture higher-level properties.

我们旨在对编码器产生并最终用于估计分割标签的特征空间进行正则化，使其利用关于部件与整体对象之间层级关系的先验知识。尤其是，我们假设该层级可由一棵以整体对象为根、以小部件为叶子的树来描述。层级的每一层都将对象分解为更小、更简单的部分。可以将此树嵌入到庞加莱球中:把整体对象的嵌入置于球心附近，把小部件的嵌入置于球面边缘附近。这由超曲率空间的性质所驱动。事实上，两点之间的测地线(最短路径)更接近球心，模拟了从一叶到另一叶沿树遍历时需经过更靠近根的路径。基于此目标，我们需要在训练中解析性地定义何为部件。一种直接的定义是若干空间上相邻的点，或由可用的部件标签所限定的子集。注意，我们的层级定义可能比可用的特定语义部件标签更通用，因此为正则化目的创建并不一定遵循标签或明确语义概念的“部件”也是合理的。本文引入了一种不同的部件定义，基于在编码器产生的特征空间中寻找随机点的 ${N}^{\prime }$ 个最近邻 $E$。该定义能够以更通用的方式捕捉“部件”，并利用可能的非局部自相似性，因为特征空间中的邻居能捕捉更高层次的属性。

HyCoRe-seg combines the classic cross-entropy loss on the outputs of the hyperbolic segmentation head with two regularizers working on the embeddings of the parts and the whole point cloud. In particular, the overall loss function for training is as follows:

HyCoRe-seg 将超曲率分割头输出上的经典交叉熵损失与作用于部件与整体点云嵌入的两个正则器相结合。具体而言，训练的总体损失函数如下:

$$
L = {L}_{CE} + \alpha {R}_{\text{ contr }} + \beta {R}_{\text{ hier }}. \tag{1}
$$

The ${R}_{\text{ hier }}$ regularizer promotes the aforementioned part-whole hierarchy by means of a triplet cost:

${R}_{\text{ hier }}$ 正则器通过三元组代价促进上述的部件—整体层级:

$$
{R}_{\text{ hier }}\left( {{\mathbf{z}}_{\text{ whole }},{\mathbf{z}}_{\text{ part }}}\right)  = \max \left( {0,{\begin{Vmatrix}{\mathbf{z}}_{\text{ whole }}\end{Vmatrix}}_{\mathbb{D}} - {\begin{Vmatrix}{\mathbf{z}}_{\text{ part }}\end{Vmatrix}}_{\mathbb{D}} + \gamma \left( {N}^{\prime }\right) }\right) .
$$

The embedding of the whole point cloud ${\mathbf{z}}_{\text{ whole }}$ is obtained by averaging the features of all points in the Euclidean space, as produced by the encoder $E$ , and then mapped to the hyperbolic space via exponential map, while the embedding of the part ${\mathbf{z}}_{\text{ whole }}$ is obtained in the same way but only restricted to the points selected as a part. We remark that we experimentally verified that average pooling in the Euclidean domain seems superior to doing that in the hyperbolic space. This might be related to the difficulty in defining the hyperbolic average operation (Einstein midpoint), as it is not available in closed form for the Poincarè ball, and the typical approach of mapping to the Klein model seems to introduce undesirable approximations.

整个点云的嵌入 ${\mathbf{z}}_{\text{ whole }}$ 通过对编码器 $E$ 在欧几里得空间中生成的所有点的特征做平均获得，然后经指数映射映射到双曲空间；部分的嵌入 ${\mathbf{z}}_{\text{ whole }}$ 以同样方式获得，但仅限于被选为该部分的点。我们注意到实验上验证到在欧几里得域中做平均池化优于在双曲空间中直接做，这可能与定义双曲平均(爱因斯坦中点)的困难有关，因为在庞加莱球模型中没有闭式解，而通常映射到克莱因模型的方法似乎引入了不希望的近似。

Additionally, the ${R}_{\text{ contr }}$ regularizer promotes correct clustering of parts and whole point clouds in the hyperbolic space. It is defined as follows:

此外，${R}_{\text{ contr }}$ 正则器促进部件与整体点云在超曲率空间中的正确聚类。其定义如下:

$$
{R}_{\text{ contr }}\left( {{\mathbf{z}}_{\text{ whole }}^{ + },{\mathbf{z}}_{\text{ part }}^{ + },{\mathbf{z}}_{\text{ part }}^{ - }}\right)  =
$$

$$
\max \left( {0,{d}_{\mathbb{D}}\left( {{\mathbf{z}}_{\text{ whole }}^{ + },{\mathbf{z}}_{\text{ part }}^{ + }}\right)  - {d}_{\mathbb{D}}\left( {{\mathbf{z}}_{\text{ whole }}^{ + },{\mathbf{z}}_{\text{ part }}^{ - }}\right)  + \delta \left( {N}^{\prime }\right) }\right)
$$

where ${\mathbf{z}}_{\text{ whole }}^{ + }$ and ${\mathbf{z}}_{\text{ part }}^{ + }$ are the hyperbolic representation of the whole and a part from the same point cloud, while ${\mathbf{z}}_{\text{ part }}^{ - }$ is the embedding of a part of a different point cloud from a different class. In both regularizers, hyperparameters $\gamma ,\delta$ are functions of the number of points ${N}^{\prime }$ , computed as $\gamma \left( {N}^{\prime }\right)  = \delta \left( {N}^{\prime }\right)  = \; {1024}/{N}^{\prime }$ .

其中 ${\mathbf{z}}_{\text{ whole }}^{ + }$ 和 ${\mathbf{z}}_{\text{ part }}^{ + }$ 是同一点云中整体与某一部件的双曲表示，${\mathbf{z}}_{\text{ part }}^{ - }$ 则是来自不同类别且属于另一点云的部件的嵌入。在两种正则项中，超参数 $\gamma ,\delta$ 是点数 ${N}^{\prime }$ 的函数，计算方式为 $\gamma \left( {N}^{\prime }\right)  = \delta \left( {N}^{\prime }\right)  = \; {1024}/{N}^{\prime }$。

### 3.2. Differences with respect to whole point cloud classi- fication

### 3.2. 与整体点云分类的不同之处

In our earlier work [5], we showed that the hyperbolic framework is very effective for the problem of whole point cloud classification. However, we need to remark one fundamental, and possibly counterintuitive, difference when addressing the point cloud part segmentation task. The classification problem in [5] defines the desired compositional hierarchy by placing whole objects as leaves of a tree and smaller parts towards the root. This hierarchy captured the idea that whole objects are specializations of simpler universal parts, and traversing the hierarchy from object A to object B requires passing through common part ancestors. This is reflected in the hyperbolic embedding by placing whole objects close to the edge of the Poincarè ball. Notice how this is exactly reversed with respect to the hierarchy designed for the segmentation task in this paper.

在我们早期的工作 [5] 中，我们证明了双曲框架在整体点云分类问题上非常有效。然而，在处理点云部件分割任务时，需要指出一个根本且可能违反直觉的差异。[5] 中的分类问题通过将整个对象置于树的叶子、将更小的部件置于靠近根的位置来定义期望的组合层次结构。该层次结构体现了整体对象是更简单通用部件的特化这一观念，从对象 A 到对象 B 的层次遍历需要经过共同的部件祖先。这在双曲嵌入中表现为将整体对象放置在 Poincarè 球边缘附近。注意这与本文为分割任务设计的层次结构恰好相反。

The reason why this description is needed to properly exploit the regularizing effect of the hierarchy is related to the geometry of the hyperbolic space as function of the task we want to solve. In the classification task, we want to separate as much as possible the clusters of embeddings of whole objects belonging to different classes. For part segmentation, we wish to do the same, but at a part level, rather than object level. This requirement is better satisfied if those embeddings are closer to edge of the Poincarè ball where space is expanding exponentially fast and more room is available to better separate clusters.

之所以需要这种描述来充分利用层次结构的正则化效果，是因为超曲面空间的几何性质会随着我们要解决的任务而变化。在分类任务中，我们希望尽可能将属于不同类别的整个物体的嵌入簇分开。对于部分分割，我们也希望达到同样的效果，但对象级别换成了部件级别。如果这些嵌入更接近庞加莱球的边缘——那里空间以指数速度扩展、拥有更多可用空间来更好地分离簇——这一要求会得到更好的满足。

## 4. 实验结果

In this section, we report the experimental results obtained by HyCoRe-seg for the part segmentation task. We use the widely-known ShapeNetPart dataset [14] composed of 16881 3D objects spread across 16 categories. We apply our method to the DGCNN architecture to observe how HyCoRe-seg can boost the performance of a recent widely-used model. The Euclidean segmentation head of the original model is replaced with a hyperbolic equivalent with a Mobius and softmax layer shared across points. The overall number of parameters is unchanged. We set $\alpha  = \beta  = {0.01},\gamma  = {1000}$ and $\delta  = 4$ . A part has ${N}^{\prime }$ points selected as nearest neighors in the feature sapce of a random point. ${N}^{\prime }$ is chosen as a random number between 200 and 600. We train the models using Riemannian SGD optimization. Pytorch and the geopt library are used for hyperbolic operations. Models are trained on Nvidia Titan RTX GPUs.

## 5. CONCLUSIONS

## 5. CONCLUSIONS

We showed how to use the hyperbolic space and hyperbolic neural network layers to improve the performance of state-of-the-art models for point cloud part segmentation. The proposed regularization framework allows to effectively capture the hierarchical nature of 3D parts thanks to the properties of the hyperbolic space. More work is needed to generalize the framework beyond objects to address tasks like scene segmentation.

我们展示了如何利用双曲空间与双曲神经网络层来提升最先进点云部件分割模型的性能。所提出的正则化框架借助双曲空间的特性，有效捕捉了 3D 部件的层级性。仍需更多工作将该框架推广到对象之外，以解决诸如场景分割等任务。
